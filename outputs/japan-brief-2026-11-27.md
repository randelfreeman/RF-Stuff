# Japan Portfolio Brief: Friday, November 27, 2026

This brief covers the **Tokyo and Seoul sessions of Fri Nov 27**. US markets were closed Thursday for Thanksgiving and have a half day today (equities close 1pm ET).

Asia had a strong risk-on finish to the week:
- **Tokyo:** the Nikkei rose 2.1% and TOPIX 1.7%. Banks gained 2.8% and non-ferrous metals 6.9%.
- **Seoul:** the KOSPI jumped 4.3% and the KOSDAQ 8.1%, on hopes for a market-stabilisation fund.
- **Tokyo CPI:** core inflation reached a 16-month high but came in just under consensus, so the December BoJ hike stays the base case without being forced.

**3. NAVER (035420): the crypto-exchange ownership-cap bill is now formally in the Assembly, and the FSC says the Dunamu deal gets no exemption. This is the main structural risk to the swap.**
- **What the bill does.** The FSC draft, released Nov 24, caps a single shareholder of a crypto exchange at **20%, or up to 34% with FSC approval**. Votes above the cap are suspended, with a 3-year grace period for existing exchanges ([Segye](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20261124516155)).
- **Where it stands.**
  - The chair of the finance committee, Yoo Dong-soo, **tabled it unchanged on Nov 26**.
  - **A public hearing is set for Dec 3.** Lawmakers from both parties have raised constitutional objections ([Asiae](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2026112617082487025)).
  - **The FSC says there is no exemption for combinations completed before the law takes effect** ([Sisa Journal-e](https://www.sisajournal-e.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=425333)).
- **The deal.** The NAVER Financial–Dunamu swap date remains **Mar 31, 2027**, with the shareholder meeting Feb 26 and a ratio of 2.5422618 ([fnnews](https://www.fnnews.com/news/202610071526186376)). The KFTC ruling is still pending and expected within the year.
- **Stock.** About ₩163,400 (+2%) intraday (*single source*, [topstarnews](https://topstarnews.net/news/articleView.html?idxno=16287639)).
- **Verdict: MATTERS.** The cap would force Dunamu's major holders to sell down within the grace period, which undermines the logic of the combined structure. The Dec 3 hearing is the next marker.
