How to read it

  • Headline EV (0.74x) sits below the line - at a 22% margin the peer line implies ~0.96x EV/Sales, EV ~£124m, ~270p per share

  • R² of 0.34 is moderate: fee mix (alternatives, EM, wealth) explains much of the residual - a cross-check, not a target
  • Closing the EV/Sales gap is worth ~45p/share; half of it ~23p
