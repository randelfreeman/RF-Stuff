BUSINESS OVERVIEW
CONTEXT
Impax at a glance - the global specialist in transition investing, halved by outflows

What it does

  • Founded 1998 by CEO Ian Simm; London HQ, US arm (ex-Pax World, acquired 2018), Dublin, Frankfurt, Hong Kong
  • Invests in the transition to a more sustainable economy - water, efficiency, grids, resource use - framed as economic opportunity, not ethics
  • FY9/26: revenue £113.1m, adj. op. profit £16.6m (~15% margin), adj. EPS 12.2p, DPS 6.7p, fee margin 48bp
  • AUM £20.05bn (Sep-26) from a £40.1bn peak (Mar-23): ~75% listed equity (systematic 13%), fixed income, private-markets infrastructure
  • Distribution: North America ~34% of revenue, BNPP-related (Lux/France) ~32%; UK now only 2% of AUM after IEM

Regression: priced on the margin line today - the value is in margin recovery
TEV/Sales (x-axis) vs EBIT margin (y-axis) - 10 UK listed asset & wealth managers
Regression (10 UK listed asset/wealth managers, Ashmore excluded as an outlier): EBIT margin = 14.69 + 7.95 x TEV/Sales, R² = 0.34. Impax's 14.1% FY+1 margin sits at the line's intercept.

How to read it

  • Headline TEV (0.47x) sits right of the line - at a 14% margin the line implies ~0x, so current earnings are not under-priced; the market ignores the recovery
  • The asymmetry: each +5ppt of margin is worth ~0.6x TEV/Sales (~£60m, ~50p/share); management targets >=30% at £27-28bn AUM
  • Cross-checks: TEV/AUM vs E/AUM (R² 0.69) fitted 0.36% vs 0.23% actual, ~110p; PBR vs ROE (R² 0.94) fair at ~87-97p
  • R² of 0.34 is moderate - fee mix and business model drive the residual; a cross-check, not a target
