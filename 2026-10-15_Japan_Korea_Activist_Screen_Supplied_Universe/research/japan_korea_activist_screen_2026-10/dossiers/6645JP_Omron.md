## 5. Balance sheet and capital
- Cash + ST investments ¥217.9bn; total debt ¥272.9bn; net debt ¥55.1bn (Jun-26).
- **JMDC (TSE:4483), a listed subsidiary:** Omron holds 35.46m shares, 54.19% ([logmi](https://finance.logmi.jp/companies/2325), third-party aggregator). JMDC market cap on 14-Oct-26 was ¥247.7bn (S&P), so the stake is worth ≈**¥134bn ≈ 11.6% of Omron's market cap**. The DSB segment (JMDC plus Omron data) reports ¥443bn of assets against FY3/26 operating profit of ¥3.6bn (≈0.8% return on assets). The auditor flagged DSB goodwill as a key audit matter; the fair-value test relies on JMDC's share price plus a control premium ([#1956](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000262413344-1956)).
- Policy shareholdings (ex-JMDC group): 2 specified holdings at ¥17.6bn plus 9 deemed holdings at ¥12.6bn ([#1253](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000262413344-1253)). S&P long-term investments: ¥13.7bn.
- Device & Module Solutions (DMB) was sold to Carlyle for ¥81bn, closing 1-Oct-2026, and Omron keeps a minority stake (S&P keydev 1992782280). Cash proceeds ≈7% of market cap.
- Buybacks: FY3/24–26 total ≈¥1.35bn (negligible). Dividend policy: DOE ≈3% ([#117](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000260027181-117)). FY3/26 payout 72% (¥104 / EPS ¥144.8). LTM ROE 4.1%. Company ROE was 3.5% and ROIC 3.0% for FY3/26.
- Real-estate unrealised gains: n/a.

## 6. Business and mismanagement evidence
- FY3/26 segments (S&P): IAB ¥410.9bn sales, 10.4% OP margin; Healthcare ¥145.4bn, 10.6%; Social Systems ¥153.2bn, 12.9%; DSB ¥51.4bn, 7.0%; corporate costs −¥21.6bn. Group OP was ¥59.9bn (7.8%), against a peer-median EBIT margin of 19.8%.
- One-offs: restructuring of ¥23.8bn in FY3/25 and ¥2.6bn in FY3/26; goodwill impairment of ¥11.7bn in FY3/25. DMB was a discontinued-ops loss of ¥5.7bn in FY3/26 (yuho P&L, [#1293](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000262413344-1293)).
- NEXT2025 restructuring (Feb-2024) cut about 2,000 jobs globally ([#179](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_T000003039427-179)).
- The 5-year TSR of −36.9% trails peers by 74pp and ETF 1625 by 155pp.
- M&A record: the JMDC TOB at ¥5,700/share (2023) now marks at ¥3,785. A $680m OnLogic/Interlogic acquisition was agreed in Oct-2026 (S&P keydev 2026110678), so DMB proceeds are being recycled into M&A rather than returned to shareholders.
