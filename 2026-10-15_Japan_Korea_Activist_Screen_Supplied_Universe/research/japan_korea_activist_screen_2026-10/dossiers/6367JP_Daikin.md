## 6. Business and mismanagement evidence
- FY3/26 segments (S&P): AC/Refrigeration ¥4,624bn, **8.2% OP margin**; Chemicals ¥307bn, 10.8%; Others ¥114bn, 4.3%. Peers Trane and Lennox earn 18–20% EBIT margins.
- Problem units named by management: the Filter business and European refrigeration ([#154](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000264540293-154)). Management will decide on exits within the first 3 years of FUSION30 ([#244](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000265935200-244)).
- Europe: the FY target OPM is only ~4–5%, and analysts flag pressure from Chinese competitors ([#407](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_T000003780314-407)).
- Capital efficiency: ROE fell from 12.3% (FY3/23) to 9.1% (FY3/26). The yuho's own 5-year TSR index for FY3/26 is 89.8, against 175.0 for the Nikkei 225 (base 100).
- Legal: a US HVAC price-fixing class action filed 20-Mar-2026 names Daikin's US subsidiaries (S&P keydev 1995688486).

## 7. Governance
- Chair and CEO are combined: **Masanori Togawa, Chairman & CEO**. He was CEO as president from Jun-2011 to 2024 and has been chairman & CEO since then (~15 years as CEO). N. Takenaka is President/COO.
- Ex-chair Noriyuki Inoue retains the "Chairman Emeritus" title (S&P).
- Board ≈10 directors, ≈5 outside, including G. Wiesheu (new Jun-2026) (S&P; verify). Kansayaku structure. A CFO role was newly established in 2026 ([#318](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_T000003723549-318)).
- Pay: the PSU is linked to D-ROIC and relative TSR vs TOPIX ([#814](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000262413584-814)). Takeover defence: none identified.
