## 6. Business & mismanagement evidence
- **Capital is inefficient.** About 26% of market cap is net cash, and ROE has stayed at or below 8.7% for five years. Management's own review of the previous plan admits "considerable room for improving asset and capital efficiency" and "inadequate IR and SR activities" ([AR FY25 #251](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000265507578-251)).
- **Overhead is high.** SG&A is about 3.6pp of sales above the peer median, although the EBIT margin (6.9%) is above the peer median (5.5%). The issue is a cash-heavy balance sheet more than poor operations.
- **A capex cycle is starting.** LTM FCF is negative, and the plan earmarks **¥35bn of growth investment**, including the Nara plant rebuild ([Call 3-Mar-2026 #269](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_T000003671312-269)). That is about 43% of market cap.
- **H1 2026:** sales ¥50.1bn (+5.8%) and net income ¥2.04bn (−16%) ([#2](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000264746845-2)).
- **Segment profitability:** n/a (not retrieved).
