# Seoul City Gas Co., Ltd. (KOSE:A017390) — OWNERSHIP: FAIL

Sector: city-gas distribution (Seoul west / Gyeonggi), with large financial and real-estate assets. Market cap ₩207.9bn (S&P, 2026-10-15) ≈ US$147m at ₩1,410/US$.

**Why it fails**
1. The largest shareholder is **Boun Co. (보운㈜, formerly 서울도시개발) at 26.27%**; Chairman Kim Young-min (김영민) owns 98.04% of Boun. Kim also holds 9.54–9.64% directly. The largest shareholder plus related parties hold **51.23%** (Sep-2026 aggregator; news reports give 51.24%). Heir Kim Yo-han (김요한) holds about 0.02%.
2. Treasury shares ≈ 20.3% (approx.: 5,000,000 shares issued vs 3,982,792 outstanding per S&P; press reported 21.3% in Mar-2025). Measured against shares outstanding excluding treasury, the group's voting share is about 64%. Activists on the register: none found.
3. **Why the price collapsed:** this was not a spin-off or a split. The stock was one of eight names (with Daesung Holdings, Samchully, Sunkwang and others) that Ra Deok-yeon's ring inflated through matched trades and CFD accounts between May 2019 and Apr 2023. Prosecutors allege ₩737.7bn of unjust gains. On 2023-04-24, CFD margin-call selling through the Société Générale desk triggered several consecutive limit-down days. S&P monthly closes: ₩89,000 (Jan-2021) → ₩173,000 (2021-10-15) → ₩496,500 (Mar-2023) → ₩127,900 (Apr-2023) → ₩66,600 (Jun-2023). Kim Young-min sold shares by block deal about a week before the crash (about ₩45.7bn reported). Daesung Holdings sold blocks of 2–3% in Aug-2022, Nov-2022, Jan-2023 and Mar-2023 (S&P transactions). The 5y TSR base is therefore a manipulated price.
4. Context only: price ₩173,000 → ₩65,300 → ₩62,400 → ₩53,100. Approx. TSR 1y −10.5%, 3y −7.2%, 5y −62.9%, against KOSPI 200 at +85.0%, +189.3% and +140.5%. LTM EBIT ₩9.8bn (0.55% margin). S&P net cash ₩661bn, about 3.2x market cap. TEV is negative (S&P TEV/EBIT −44.9x, n/m); ND/EBITDA −13x (screen). Governance flags in the press: two years of operating losses alongside record profits at the third generation's private company, related-party "일감" deals, and a dividend called "황제배당".
5. Conclusion: the company is a deep-value cash shell, but the family controls 51%, plus about 20% treasury stock, so it fails the gate.
