## 5. Balance sheet and capital
- Cash + ST investments ¥126.9bn; debt ¥23.5bn; **net cash ¥103.4bn ≈ 19% of market cap**. Equity ¥493.1bn.
- Investment securities (S&P long-term investments) ¥56.5bn (Jun-26).
- **Yamaha Motor cross-holding:**
  - Sep-2024: Yamaha sold 18m Yamaha Motor shares, taking its stake from 4.47% to 2.75%.
  - 26-Aug-2026: it sold 14m more, from 2.84% to **1.46%** (14.93m shares left). This booked a ¥24.9bn non-consolidated gain; under IFRS there is no consolidated P&L gain ([Yamaha release](https://www.yamaha.com/en/news_release/files/news/26082601/pdf/2608260101.pdf)).
  - Yamaha Motor still holds 3.20% of Yamaha.
- Real estate: resort facilities (Fukuroi etc.) on 1,796k m² of land, booked at ¥0.9bn land plus ¥3.2bn total ([#757](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000262478019-757)). Unrealised gain: n/a.
- Buybacks: ¥18.3bn (FY3/24), ¥49.1bn (FY3/25), ¥15.0bn (FY3/26) = ¥82.4bn over 3 years.
- FY3/26 payout 49% (¥26 / EPS ¥52.7). LTM ROE 6.5%. MTP return target: total return ≥50% cumulative ([#792](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000262478019-792)).

## 6. Business and mismanagement evidence
- FY3/26 segments (S&P): Musical Instruments ¥304.9bn, **7.0% OP margin**; Audio ¥142.7bn, 7.6%; Others ¥18.0bn, a small loss. Group OP ¥32.2bn (6.9%) on sales of ¥465bn, which were flat for 3 years (¥462.9bn, ¥462.1bn, ¥465.3bn).
- **China piano collapse:** acoustic-piano education demand shrank on the property slump and the "double reduction" policy ([#542](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000243195096-542)). Piano-plant impairments in China and Indonesia were ¥3.2bn (FY3/24) and ¥10.4bn (FY3/25) ([#420](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000243226644-420)), plus ≈¥3.5bn of Indonesian severance.
- Audio: one-off clean-up costs in Q4 FY3/25, and European pro-audio demand slowed in FY3/26 (S&P keydevs).
- The 5-year TSR of −45% trails peers by 73pp and the ETF by 106pp. Year-1 net income of ¥23.7bn is about 59% of the run-rate implied by the MTP's ¥120bn 3-year net income plan (approx.: ¥120bn / 3) ([#42](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000264802377-42)).

## 7. Governance
- Company with three committees (nominating structure). 8 directors, **6 independent (75%)** (S&P).
- Chair (T. Nakata, ex-CEO 2013–24) is separate from CEO (Atsushi Yamaura, CEO since Apr-2024).
- An outside director rotated in Jun-2026 (Waring in, Candland out). Pay KPIs are ROIC and TSR ([#1502](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000262478019-1502)).
- No family control. Takeover defence: none identified. The shareholder perk plan was abolished in Jul-2024.
