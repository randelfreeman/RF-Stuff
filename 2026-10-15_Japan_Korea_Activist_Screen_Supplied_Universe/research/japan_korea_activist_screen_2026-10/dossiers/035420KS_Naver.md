- **Other holdings:** NAVER Financial 89.21% (consolidated). Proton Parent (Poshmark) is carried at ₩1.74tn standalone. There is a cross-shareholding with Mirae Asset Securities from a treasury-share swap, with ROFR/put terms.
- **Buybacks and cancellations:** ₩405bn of buybacks in FY2024. Cancellations were 1.64m (Nov-23), 1.62m (Aug-24), 2.35m (Nov-24, newly bought), 1.58m (Aug-25) and 4.90m (Aug-26) shares.
- **Payout:** the FY2025 dividend was ₩393.6bn (₩2,630/share), 20% of FY25 NI of ₩1.95tn. The FY2025–27 policy is 25–35% of the 2-year average FCF, paid as dividends or cancellations.
- **Returns and dilution:** LTM ROE is 6.9%. The **NVIDIA placement of ₩1.48tn** is at ₩204,500, 14% above NOW.

## 6. Business and mismanagement evidence
- **The de-rating is not driven by earnings.** Revenue rose from ₩6.82tn (FY21) to ₩12.04tn (FY25) and OP from ₩1.33tn to ₩2.21tn. The stock fell 54% over the same period.
- **AI capex is compressing FCF.**
  - Q4-25 FCF was ₩185bn, down ₩252.8bn YoY. Q2-26 FCF was ₩46.3bn, down ₩150.5bn YoY.
  - The NVIDIA deal requires NAVER to secure at least US$9bn of compute infrastructure, excluding the placement proceeds. Planned capacity is a 200MW "AI factory", and GPUs are being funded through a financing arrangement with Brookfield.
  - LTM capex is 13% of revenue.
- **Undisciplined M&A:**
  - Poshmark (acquired 2023) goodwill impairment was a key audit matter. FY2024 goodwill impairment was ₩137.6bn across the Poshmark, Wattpad and Munpia CGUs (split n/a).
  - There is a long tail of small minority stakes: Kurly 4.91% (₩43bn), InBody 8.5% (₩32.5bn), note inc. 7.9% (₩18.4bn) and others.
- **NAVER Financial–Dunamu share exchange:**
  - The ratio is 1 Dunamu share to 2.5422618 NF shares (NF valued at ₩172,780, Dunamu at ₩439,252).
  - After the exchange NAVER's NF stake falls from 89% to ~17%. Proxies from Song Chi-hyung (19.5%) and Kim Hyung-nyun (10.0%) still give it 46.5% of the votes.
  - *Inference:* minority holders give up most of the fintech economics in exchange for keeping control.
  - The deal can be terminated if appraisal claims reach ₩1.2tn or more at either company.
- **Equity-method exposure:** LTM NI includes associates such as A Holdings. *Inference:* P/E overstates core earnings power.

## 7. Governance
- **Board:** 7 directors (2 inside, 1 non-executive, 4 independent, i.e. 57% independent). Chair and CEO roles are separated, and the audit committee is all independent.
- **Leadership:**
  - Lee Hae-jin returned as board chair (term Mar-2025 to Mar-2028).
  - CEO Choi Soo-yeon was appointed Mar-2022 and re-appointed to Mar-2028.
  - *Inference:* the founder's influence through the chair seat is far larger than his 3.8% stake.
- **Regulatory status:** designated a large business group (cross-shareholding restricted) on 1-May-2025.
- **Executive pay:** RSUs vest at 0–200% depending on TSR relative to KOSPI 200 companies.
- **Value-up:** governance KPIs and the 3-year return policy are disclosed. A formal Value-up plan document is n/a in the sources read.

## 9. Likely management defence (inference)
- AI and data-centre capex is existential ("actionable AI", sovereign AI). Returns are already linked to FCF at 25–35%.
- NAVER has retired 12.1m shares since Nov-2023 (5 cancellations; approx. 7% of the issued shares at the time), and the residual treasury is only for compensation.
- NVIDIA paid a premium to NOW, which validates the AI factory strategy. Dunamu creates a "₩20tn fintech" while NAVER keeps control.
- The board has an independent majority and separate chair and CEO, so there is no governance gap.
