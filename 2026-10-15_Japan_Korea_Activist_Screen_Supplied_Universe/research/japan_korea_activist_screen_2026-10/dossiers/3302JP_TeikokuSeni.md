# Teikoku Sen-i Co., Ltd. (TSE:3302) — OWNERSHIP: PASS

## 1. Header
- **What it does:** disaster-prevention equipment, including fire hoses, rescue gear, CBRNE equipment, rescue and airport crash-tender vehicles, "Hydro-Sub" high-volume pumping and airport security scanners. It also has a linen and technical-textiles business and a small rental-property business.
- **Sector:** TSE Textiles, which maps to TOPIX-17 Raw Materials & Chemicals (ETF **1620**). FY ends December.
- **Market cap:** ¥75.1bn (S&P, 2026-10-14), about **US$476m** at ¥158/US$. TEV ¥49.6bn.

## 5. Balance sheet and capital (Jun-2026 unless noted)
- **Cash:** ¥24.05bn cash plus ¥1.50bn short-term securities. Debt is ¥34m, so **net cash ≈ ¥25.5bn, 34% of market cap**. Equity ratio 80.7%.
- **Policy shareholdings (Dec-2025):** 14 listed names worth ¥36.91bn and 13 unlisted names worth ¥0.08bn. None were sold in FY25.
- **Hulic:** 17,006,292 shares, carried at ¥29.16bn. At ¥1,665 (2026-10-14) that is ≈¥28.3bn, **≈38% of market cap**. The stated rationale is "business relationships in disaster prevention and real estate"; the quantitative benefit is undisclosed "for confidentiality".
- **Cash plus securities:** ≈¥62bn, about **83% of market cap** before tax and ≈69% after the deferred tax liability.
- **Investment income:** dividends and interest of ¥1.22bn in FY25, ≈23% of pretax profit.
- **Real estate:** land at book value ¥3.99bn. The rental segment earned FY25 revenue of ¥0.55bn and OP of ¥0.41bn. Teikoku also holds 29% of 麻業会館 (office leasing). Fair value: n/a.
- **Dividends:** DPS rose from ¥45 (FY21) to ¥55 (FY25), with ¥65 guided for FY26. FY25 payout was 37.8% and total payout 69.9%.
- **Buybacks:** FY22 ¥0.60bn, FY23 ¥0.09bn, FY24 nil, FY25 ¥1.54bn, H1-26 nil. The FY25 figure includes ¥1.18bn approved on the same day NAVF's proposals were disclosed.
- **Return target:** total payout of ≈50% under the Teisen 2028 plan.
- **ROE:** 5.4% in FY25 (secondary source); ≈6.4% LTM.

## 6. Business and capital-allocation evidence
- **FY25 segments:**
  - Disaster prevention: ¥27.26bn sales (81%), OP ¥4.30bn (15.8% margin).
  - Textiles: ¥5.92bn sales, OP ¥0.99bn.
  - Rental: OP ¥0.41bn.
  - Corporate costs: −¥1.64bn.
- **Idle balance sheet:** **unallocated corporate assets of ¥53.4bn are 58% of total assets.**
- **Five years of no growth:** sales went from ¥32.3bn (FY20) to ¥33.6bn (FY25), +4%; OP went from ¥4.19bn to ¥4.06bn.
- **Missed targets:** the Teisen 2025 targets (OP ≥¥5.0bn, ordinary profit ≥¥6.0bn) were missed. FY25 guidance (sales ¥36.5bn, OP ¥4.8bn) was missed by 8% and 16%.
- **Momentum now:** the backlog stood at ¥23.85bn at end-FY25, against ¥18.43bn a year earlier. H1-26 sales rose 30.5% and OP 53.4% to ¥4.07bn.
- **Targets look unambitious:** LTM OP of ¥5.47bn is already near the **Teisen 2028 FY2028 target of ≥¥5.8bn**. The unchanged FY26 OP guidance of ¥4.3bn implies second-half OP of only ≈¥0.2bn. That this is sandbagging is an inference; H2 is seasonally weak.
- **Lean overhead:** SG&A is 13.9% of sales against a peer median of 21.8%. Capital allocation is the issue, not costs.
- **M&A:** no material acquisitions; the insurance agency was sold in FY24. Headcount is 362.

## 7. Governance
- **Board:** 7 directors, 3 of them independent (**43%**). Statutory auditor board of 3, 2 of them outside. Directors serve one-year terms. **The CEO chairs the board.** The nomination and remuneration committee is chaired by an outside director.
- **Chairman & CEO Tsuyoshi Shiraiwa:** **age 80**, ex-Fuji Bank, top executive since 2012 (≈14 years). He owns 40k shares plus 231k under the director stock trust.
- **Other executives:** President & COO Toru Masutani is 78. EVP Tatsuru Okamura is ex-Mizuho.
- **Fuyo/Mizuho web:**
  - Outside director Fukasawa (86) is an ex-chairman of Yasuda Real Estate.
  - The standing auditor is ex-Mizuho Trust.
  - Outside auditor Nishiwaki is ex-Sompo; Sompo Japan holds 3.54%.
  - Outside auditor **Kinoshita is a sitting Hulic executive officer.**
- **Takeover defences:** no rights plan, only a "basic policy". The articles cut the quorum for special resolutions to one-third, and the board can authorise buybacks.
- **TSE cost-of-capital disclosure:** P/B fell from 1.72x (2014) to 0.82x (2023). Management recognises a cost of capital of ≈5%, against 7.8% cited by investors (secondary source).

## 9. Likely management defence (inference)
- A buyback "does not contribute to long-term corporate value enhancement"; the company prefers to buy back "flexibly" (board opinion, Feb-2026).
- Given the company's size, "the current outside-officer structure ensures quick decisions and oversight" (有報).
- Cash is needed for Teisen 2028 alliances or M&A and for lumpy government vehicle orders. The Hulic stake supports business ties. DPS is up 18% and the total-payout target is 50%.
- With ≈26% of shares in stable hands and two defeated proxy rounds, management will claim a mandate.
