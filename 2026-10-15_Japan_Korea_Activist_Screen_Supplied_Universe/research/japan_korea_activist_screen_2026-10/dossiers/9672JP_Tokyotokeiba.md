- Market cap: ¥124.9bn (US$0.79bn at 158 JPY/USD); TEV ¥119.4bn (S&P, 2026-10-15)

  The amusement and services businesses are sub-scale.
- **Related-party landlord conflict.** The Special Ward association pays **¥10.55bn of rent (25% of revenue)**, a related-party transaction, while being the 14% shareholder. The tenant effectively helps set its own rent.
- **¥160bn Oi redevelopment (decided 2026-09-30):**
  - Scope: a new Ichihara training centre in Chiba (1,200-stall stables, tracks) and new or renovated racecourse facilities.
  - Timing: construction 2027/28–2033.
  - Funding: internal funds and borrowing.
  - Scale: **1.28x market cap and 1.63x equity.** Lease terms and expected returns are not disclosed (n/a).
- **Growth driver:** SPAT4 online betting drove the FY25 upgrade (OP guidance raised to ¥15.0bn; actual OP ¥15.4bn). FY26 guidance is OP ¥15.8bn and NI ¥10.8bn.

## 9. Likely management defence (inference)
- The business is of an "extremely public nature", so management will say it calls for a long-term stable foundation and stable dividends rather than leverage or payouts.
- They will say the redevelopment is needed to sustain Ohi racing (and therefore the rent), and that returns are protected by the long-term lease to public bodies.
- Payout has already been raised (35%, DPS ¥146) and buybacks with cancellation are being done.
- The 45.6% public-sector bloc, which shares these priorities, gives management a secure base.
