# Daehan Flour Mills Co., Ltd. (KOSE:A001130) — OWNERSHIP: FAIL (borderline)

Sector: food / flour milling (brand "곰표"), plus feed and family affiliates. Market cap ₩183.8bn (S&P, 2026-10-15) ≈ US$130m at ₩1,410/US$.

**Why it fails**
1. The largest shareholder is unlisted family vehicle **D&B Company (디앤비컴퍼니) at 27.82%**. Chairman Lee Keon-young (이건영) holds 7.01% and his brother Lee Jae-young (이재영) 2.32%. The largest shareholder plus 17 specially related parties hold **42.33%** (as of Jun-2025, news.tf.co.kr; the same figure appears on the Sep-2026 aggregator). The family owns 84.01% of D&B Company; its largest owner is Lee Hye-young (이혜영, the chairman's elder sister) with 21.6%. Ownership sits with the sister and management with the brother.
2. 42.33% is only 2.3pp above the 40% gate, so this is a borderline FAIL. Any family sell-down (tax, succession) could bring it below 40%.
3. Treasury shares ≈ 2.4% (approx.: 16.9m shares issued after the 10:1 split vs 16,498,540 outstanding per S&P). No institutional activist fund found. Minority shareholders tabled a stock split and a share buyback at the Mar-2026 AGM. The split was adopted (10:1, effective 2026-04-22 per S&P); the buyback was voted down. The NTS has opened a special tax audit of D&B Company and 리빙소프트 (Feb-2026 reports). Also on record: a flour-price collusion fine, and reports of affiliate losses being funded from the core business.
4. Context only: price (split-adjusted) ₩16,250 → ₩12,440 → ₩14,260 → ₩11,190 (2021-10-15 / 2023-10-13 / 2025-10-15 / 2026-10-14). Approx. TSR 1y −18.7%, 3y −2.0%, 5y −21.9%, against KOSPI 200 at +85.0%, +189.3% and +140.5%. LTM EBIT ₩45.6bn; S&P net cash ₩83bn; TEV/EBIT 1.9x; ND/EBITDA −1.0x (screen).
5. Conclusion: control is entrenched below 50%, but the family group holds 42.33%, so the stock fails the gate. Worth re-screening if the stake drops below 40%; minority-shareholder pressure is already visible.
