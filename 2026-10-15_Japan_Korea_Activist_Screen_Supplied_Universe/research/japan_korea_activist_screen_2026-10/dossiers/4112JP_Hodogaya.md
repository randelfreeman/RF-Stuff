## 5. Business and mismanagement evidence

**Segment results, FY3/26:**

| Segment | Revenue | Operating profit | Comment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Functional colorants (OLED via SFC, imaging) | ¥26.3bn | ¥3.51bn | Core profit engine |
| Functional resins | ¥7.95bn | −¥0.59bn | Loss-making; was −¥0.05bn in FY3/25 |
| Basic chemicals | ¥7.41bn | ¥0.32bn | |
| Agroscience | ¥5.11bn | ¥0.07bn | Sub-scale |
| Logistics | ¥3.21bn | ¥0.36bn | |

- **No earnings growth:** revenue went from ¥41.2bn (FY3/21) to ¥48.0bn (FY3/26), while net income went from ¥3.25bn (FY3/22) to ¥3.05bn (FY3/26). The five-year TSR of −11.5% compares with a peer median of +121%.
- **Guidance misses:** operating profit guidance was cut from ¥5.0bn to ¥3.5bn on weak OLED sales (Nov-2025). FY3/27 guidance is ¥3.4bn.
- **Large acquisition:** Framochem Kft (Hungary), bought from SK Capital's VanDeMark for ¥16.0bn in cash, which is about 50% of market cap. Target earnings were not disclosed in the sources we saw.
- **New venture:** a nucleic-acid CDMO subsidiary (NuChord, Sep-2026) and a new building at the Koriyama plant. This is capital-hungry diversification while the core is under-earning.
- **Concentration and legal risk at SFC:** Samsung Display is about 75% of SFC's revenue. SFC also faces LG Chem patent litigation, including a ₩30bn damages claim.
- **SG&A:** 18.4% of sales vs a peer median of 14.8%.
