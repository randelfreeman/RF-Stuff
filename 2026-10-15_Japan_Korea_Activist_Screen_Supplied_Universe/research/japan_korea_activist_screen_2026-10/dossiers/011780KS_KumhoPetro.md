# Kumho Petro Chemical (KOSE:A011780): activist dossier, as of 15 Oct 2026

## 1. Header
- **Sector:** Commodity and specialty chemicals (synthetic rubber/NB latex, synthetic resins, phenol derivatives via Kumho P&B, energy)
- **Market cap:** ₩2.70tn, which is **US$1.92bn at ₩1,410/US$** (S&P, 15-Oct-2026). TEV ₩2.62tn.
- **OWNERSHIP: PASS.**
  - The largest single holder is Park Chul-wan at 10.33%.
  - The Park Chan-koo family holds 18.08%.
  - The DART "largest shareholder and specially related parties" total is 26.36%, and it includes both family branches. Even that total is below 40%.

## 5. Balance sheet and capital (Jun-2026)
- **Liquidity:** cash ₩653bn and ST investments ₩296bn, ₩949bn in total.
- **Debt:** ST borrowings ₩208bn, LTD ₩535bn and leases ₩120bn. S&P net debt is −₩85bn.
- **Long-term investments: ₩1.57tn, 58% of market cap.**
  - They include the stake from the 2021 OCI treasury-share swap: ₩31.5bn each way, giving Kumho 1.25% of OCI and OCI 0.56% of Kumho. Park Chul-wan's injunction against OCI's voting rights was rejected.
  - The OCI Holdings share price has roughly tripled in 2026 (Daum, Jun-2026).
  - The remaining composition is n/a.
- **Treasury and cancellations:**
  - About 10.5% remains in treasury after cancelling 875k (Mar-24), 875k (Mar-25), 874,417 (Mar-26, ₩106.5bn) and 237,628 (Sep-26) shares.
  - The "50% of existing treasury over 3 years" plan is complete. The disposition of the remaining 2.62m shares is undecided (FETV).
- **Buybacks:** ₩150bn (FY22), ₩100bn (FY23), ₩50bn (FY24) and ₩50bn (FY25).
- **Dividends paid:** ₩281bn (FY22), falling to ₩57.5bn (FY25).
- **Payout:**
  - Company-stated: FY24 dividends 22% plus cancellation 19% (41%); FY25 dividends 25% plus cancellation 17% (42%), both on separate NI.
  - Consolidated approx.: FY25 DPS of ₩1,700 × 22.5m shares ≈ ₩38bn, ~13% of consolidated NI of ₩291bn (common only).
- **ROE:** LTM 7.5%. P/B is 0.40x.

## 6. Business and mismanagement evidence
- **Cycle:**
  - Revenue went from ₩8.46tn (FY21) to ₩6.92tn (FY25), and OP from ₩2.41tn to ₩272bn (FY24 and FY25 each).
  - Q2-26 OP rebounded to ₩339bn (+420% YoY) on revenue of ₩2.27tn.
  - Synthetic rubber earned ₩189.8bn, the best since 3Q21, as NB latex prices rose on raw-material supply disruption. Kumho P&B swung to a ₩55.1bn profit.
  - Brokers expect Q3 to fall sharply on negative lagging effects.
- **Overhead is not the issue.** SG&A is 3.6% of revenue, against LG Chem 15.4%, Lotte 5.1%, Hanwha 10.4% and Zeon 16.1%.
- **The issue is capital allocation:**
  - ₩949bn of cash plus ₩1.57tn of LT investments equals 93% of market cap.
  - FCF yield is 12.5%, yet the stock trades at 0.40x book.
  - About 10.5% treasury is still uncancelled.
  - Kumho was excluded from the K-Value-up index (Sep-2024, MTN).
- **Relative performance:** Kumho beat the Korean petchem peers, which are levered and loss-making, but trailed KOSPI 200 by 163pp over 5y.

## 7. Governance
- **Board:** after the 26-Mar-2026 AGM (49th), **10 directors: 2 inside and 8 outside (80%)**, up from 70%. All 5 agenda items passed. The new audit-committee outside director is Yang Jung-won; the new outside directors are Christine Kim Jae-hee and Park Soon-ae.
- **Chair/CEO split:** n/a in the sources read. In 2022 Park Chul-wan attacked the lack of separation.
- **Family influence:** Chairman Park Chan-koo, President Park Joon-kyung and VP Park Joo-hyung run the company. Succession is under way through on-market buying by the third generation.
- **Takeover defences:** the OCI cross-holding ("white knight") and the large treasury block, which has no vote today but has been a dispute flashpoint.
- **NPS (9.64%)** has historically been the casting vote.
- **Value-up plan (11-Feb-2025):**
  - Revenue CAGR of 6% to 2030.
  - ROE of 7% by 2026 and 10% by 2030.
  - Shareholder returns of up to 40% of separate NI: dividends of 20–25% plus buybacks/cancellations of 10–15%.

## 9. Likely management defence (inference)
- Management has delivered: it cancelled 50% of treasury as promised, returns 40–42% of separate NI, and has the cleanest balance sheet among Korean petchem peers.
- It will argue that keeping net cash through the trough (peer ND/EBITDA median 6.8x) is prudent, and that retaining capital is needed to grow specialty products and the battery/eco businesses toward ROE of 10% by 2030.
- An 80% outside-director board and AGM wins of 74–76% show shareholder support.
- It will say the remaining treasury shares will be used "flexibly" (M&A or partnerships) within the law.
