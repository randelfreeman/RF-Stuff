# Komeri Co., Ltd. (TSE:8218) — OWNERSHIP: FAIL (Sasage family group ≈42–44%)

Sector: rural home-centre chain (TOPIX-17 Retail, ETF 1630). Market cap ¥177.8bn (S&P, 2026-10-14), about US$1,125m at ¥158/US$. TEV ¥196.7bn. Screen data: ND/EBITDA 0.51x, EBIT margin 6.3%.

**Why it fails**
1. **The founder family's vehicles plus the CEO hold ≥42.3% of shares excluding treasury** (有報 FY3/2026, register at 31-Mar-2026). The components are Yoneri Co. (株式会社米利, family asset company, Sanjo) at 34.2%, CEO Yuichiro Sasage (捧雄一郎) at 2.9%, Sasage Ltd. (有限会社ささげ) at 2.6%, and the Setsuryosha art foundation (公益財団法人美術育成財団雪梁舎, same address as Yoneri) at 2.6%. A change report dated 2026-05-01 adds Miho Sasage at 1.36% of issued and Miyoe Sasage at 0.18% as joint holders of Yoneri and the foundation. Miyoe's joint-holder group alone holds 18,184,336 shares, 33.79% of issued (stated purpose: "founding family, stable shareholder"). Including Miho and Miyoe, the family total is **≈44% of shares outstanding** (approx.: ≈20.8m of 47.26m shares) and ≈38.6% of issued shares including treasury. Yoneri's stake has risen steadily, from 25.2% in Mar-2016 to 34.2% in Mar-2026.

3. **Treasury:** 6,546,024 shares, 12.2% of 53,809,168 issued. 600k treasury shares were cancelled on 2025-11-14. Further cancellation or buybacks would raise the family above 45%. **Activists:** none found on the register or in large-holding reports for 2023–26.
4. **Context only (S&P closes ¥2,655 / ¥3,030 / ¥3,090 / ¥3,785):** approx. TSR 1y +24.3%, 3y +30.3%, 5y +52.2%, against TOPIX +29.0%, +78.8% and +105.2%. DPS used, FY Mar: FY22 ¥46, FY23 ¥48, FY24 ¥52, FY25 ¥54, FY26 ¥56. Peers (DCM, Kohnan, Nafco, Arcland) were not computed.
5. **Judgement:** the family plus its vehicles and its foundation exceed 40% of shares outstanding, and the stake has been creeping up for ten years. Adding the friendly Daishi Hokuetsu Bank and the employee association brings the group to ≈47%. FAIL.
