# TOC Co., Ltd. (TSE:8841) — Real estate (Gotanda/Osaki TOC buildings) — OWNERSHIP: FAIL

Mkt cap ≈ ¥82bn (≈ US$0.52bn at 158 JPY/USD; S&P CIQ screening figure supplied by caller, not re-pulled). Data date: register as of 31-Mar-2026 (有価証券報告書 filed 25-Jun-2026).

1. **Otani group holds ~52% of shares ex-treasury, above the 40% gate.** The register lists New Otani 24.08%, Otani Kosan (有限会社大谷興産) 16.56%, Otani Fund 7.85% and Otani Kosan TO 3.46%, which sum to **51.95%**. New Otani is formally TOC's "other affiliated company" (その他の関係会社): it holds 24.1% of the votes, and TOC holds 8.4% of New Otani in return. Treasury shares are 5,631,869 (6.00% of issued). The rest of the top 10 is Shinryo 5.06%, Master Trust 3.77%, Nippon Life 3.18%, Mizuho 2.41%, Yuki Nakamoto 1.86% and Toei 1.72%; together the top 10 hold 70.00%. [Yuho FY3/26](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$DOCID_NONSEC000262467922) ([#288](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000262467922-288), [#76](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000262467922-76))
2. **The founding family runs the company.** President Takuo Otani (大谷卓男) has been Representative Director & President since June 1993, which is 33 years. A sitting Representative Director & President of Hotel New Otani joined TOC's board in June 2026. Only 3 directors are outside directors ([#399](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000262467922-399)).
3. **The value gap is large.** Rental real estate has a book value of ¥59.1bn against a **fair value of ¥189.1bn** (31-Mar-2026), an unrealised gain of about ¥130bn before tax. That gain alone is about 1.6x market cap ([#684](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000262467922-684)). On top of that, the company holds cash of ¥31.1bn and investment securities of ¥27.4bn, and its equity ratio is 85.2%. FY3/26 ROE was 2.3% and DPS has been ¥10 flat for five years. TOC's own 5-year TSR is 109.0%, against 205.3% for the TOPIX real-estate index ([#6](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000262467922-6), [#250](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000262467922-250)).
4. **There is recent capital-return activity.** TOC bought back 5.45m shares for ¥3.6bn in FY3/25 and cancelled 1.43m shares in August 2023 ([#630](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000262467922-630)). No activist appears on the top-10 register. AGM vote results: n/a.
5. **Verdict: no deep dive.** A family block of about 52% means no outside campaign can win a vote. The NAV discount is extreme, so this name only works as a family-led MBO/take-private situation, not as an activist target.

Caveats: Market cap and US$ value are the caller's S&P screening data and were not recomputed. Otani Kosan and Otani Fund are treated as family vehicles because of their names and shared addresses (Kioicho 4-1 / TOC HQ); this was not verified with a 大量保有報告書. Under a stricter reading, New Otani alone at 24.1% plus Otani Kosan at 16.6% still exceeds 40%.
