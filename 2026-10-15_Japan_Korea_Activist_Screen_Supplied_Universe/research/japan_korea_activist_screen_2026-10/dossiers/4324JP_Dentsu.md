## 6. Business & mismanagement evidence
- **International M&A write-downs:**
  - Impairment losses were ¥235.3bn in FY24 and ¥402.6bn in FY25, including a ¥310.1bn goodwill charge in Q4-25.
  - Net loss attributable was −¥10.7bn (FY23), −¥192.2bn (FY24) and −¥327.6bn (FY25).
- **Japan vs International:**
  - Japan grew 6.2% organically in FY25 at a 24.4% underlying margin. The group margin was 14.4% (underlying operating profit ¥172.5bn).
  - Japan net revenue was ≈¥495.6bn of ¥1,197.5bn. On that basis, International plus HQ earned ≈¥51.6bn on ≈¥702bn, about 7.4% (approx., derived from quarterly regional net revenue × 24.4%).
- **Failed exit:**
  - The FT reported in Aug-2025 that Dentsu was exploring a sale of the international business.
  - By Jan-2026 Apollo and the rival agencies had dropped out; Bain remained "with significant reservations".
  - The sale was abandoned on 13 Feb 2026, and the shares fell 11% on the reports.
- **Restructuring:**
  - 3,400 job cuts were planned, of which 2,100 were done in FY25.
  - FY25: ¥20bn of one-off cost produced ¥14bn of savings. FY26: ¥26bn of one-off cost is expected to produce ¥42bn of annual savings.
  - Overseas entities fell from more than 1,000 (2021) to about half (Jan-2026). A further 70–80 entities are to be removed in FY26.
  - China and Australia, loss-making since 2023, turned profitable at the underlying level in FY25.
- **Index exits:** S&P Global 1200 and S&P TOPIX 150 (effective 2026-06-22).
- **Guidance and current trading:**
  - FY26 guidance: revenue ¥1,491.5bn, OP ¥152.6bn, net profit ¥78.7bn.
  - H1-26 net income was ¥46.3bn, against a loss of ¥73.6bn a year earlier.

## 7. Governance
- **Structure:** company with a nominating committee. 11 directors, 8 independent (73%).
- **Chair:** independent chair Gan Matsui, who replaced Tim Andree in Mar-2025.
- **CEO:** Takeshi Sano since 2026-03-27 (joined in 1992; previously CEO of dentsu Japan). Igarashi was CEO from 2022 to 2026.
- **Other executives:** CFO Shigeki Endo (formerly at Accenture/GE) since Feb-2025. Igarashi, Soga and Malegori all left the management team in Mar-2026.
- **Targets:** the Feb-2025 MTP set ROE in the mid-teens and a 16–17% operating margin by FY27. Part of these was withdrawn in Feb-2026; the 16% margin target is kept.

## 9. Likely management defence (inference)
- "Restoring competitiveness and profitability" is already under way with a new CEO and cost savings of about ¥50bn by 2027.
- A dividend is legally impossible while the distributable amount is negative. The priority is to rebuild the balance sheet and resume dividends "at the earliest possible timing".
- The sale process tested the market and no buyer came forward at an acceptable value. Local partnerships remain open.
- Japan is growing at 6% with a 24% margin, which management will argue does not support a break-up.
