## 6. Business & mismanagement evidence
- **Segments, FY Mar-26:**
  - Matsumotokiyoshi group: revenue ¥711.4bn, OP ¥60.8bn (**8.5%**).
  - Cocokara fine group: revenue ¥390.0bn, OP ¥23.5bn (**6.0%**). The 2.5pp margin gap is unfinished merger synergy.
  - "And Company" (other businesses): revenue ¥12.9bn, OP ¥0.2bn. Sub-scale.
- **Merger targets:** the 2021 merger targets (¥30bn synergy, OP margin ≥7%, ROE ≥10%) were hit early ([MTP](https://www.matsukiyococokara.com/ir/plan/pdf/medium_term_management_plan_20250509.pdf)).
- **New mid-term plan (to FY Mar-31):** sales ¥1.3tn + "α" from an "alliance" (連合体構想, i.e. M&A), **EBITDA margin ≥13%** (LTM 9.8%), ROE ≥12%.
- **Small deals:** Shinsho Pharmacy, Iwasaki Kokendo (merged Apr-26), John Masters Holdings (Aug-26), venture stakes (Metagen, Emol). There are no large write-downs.
- **Guidance:** FY Mar-27 OP ¥87.5bn (+3% vs FY26 ¥84.9bn). Q1 OP +8.5% year on year.
- **Overhead:** SG&A at 25.8% of sales vs a 19.1% peer median. This reflects a cosmetics and inbound-tourism mix with higher gross margin; it is not obviously bloat.
