## C. How to read the totals (avoid double-counting)

- **Cash actually returned = dividends + buyback *purchases*.** A *cancellation* retires shares the company **already bought** — it is an accounting retirement of treasury stock, **not a new cash outflow**. So do **not** add the ₩3.4bn/₩3.45bn cancellations on top of the ₩5bn buyback as separate cash.
- **Identifiable cash returned by 078070 over the window:** dividends ~**₩6.1bn** (FY2024–25 confirmed; FY2021–23 unquantified) + buybacks ≥**₩5.0bn** (2021–22 program; 2022 program size n/d) → **≈ ₩11bn+ of confirmed cash return**, understated because several years' dividends and the 2022 buyback size are undisclosed here.
- **Shares retired:** ~545,929 (3.10%) in 2026 + the 2025 tranche → the buyback-and-cancel is **recurring, ~annual**, shrinking the ~16.5–17.6m share count each year.

## D. Takeaway
Ubiquoss Holdings uses **all three** levers — a **rising, policy-anchored dividend** (₩130→₩240, 70% of adjusted NI), **trust-based buybacks**, and **annual treasury cancellations** (~₩3.4bn in 2025 *and* 2026). This is a **genuine, repeated** shareholder-return program that materially softens the "cash-hoarding control vehicle" bear point from the main note — though the absolute amounts remain modest relative to the group's asset base, and the pre-2023 dividend record is thin.
