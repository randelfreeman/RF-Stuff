## 7. Founder vs professional manager: input for assessment

- **The CEO is a professional manager, not an owner.**
  - Shayakhmetova is a career banker: Western-trained (Rutgers MBA), from the ABN AMRO Kazakhstan franchise, then Halyk corporate lending head (2004–09).
  - She holds less than 1% (board-level disclosure; her actual holding was not found).
  - She has been in place since January 2009, through three crises (2009, 2014–15, 2022) and the KKB merger.
- **Owner-operator dynamic.**
  - Control sits with ALMEX (the Kulibayevs), exercised through the AGM and one board seat held by ALMEX's own CEO (Nurabayev, at ALMEX since 2003).
  - The Kulibayevs are not executives and do not sit on the board.
  - Owner priorities appear to be dividends (Forbes.kz coverage of "Kulibayev dividends"), share liquidity, and keeping at least 51% control.
- **Board chair profile.** The two chairs since 2023 (Pavlov, then Dunayev) are both former Finance Ministers / senior state officials. This suggests the board's chair role is partly about government relations.
- **Execution is professionalised.** The bank hires foreign and specialist managers: Polish CRO Maszczyk, IT head Khasin, and B2C head Bachvarov.
