## 6. Business and mismanagement evidence
- Operating margin collapse:
  - FY3/21 7.2%, FY3/22 6.0%, FY3/23 3.3%, FY3/24 3.9%, FY3/25 3.5%, FY3/26 2.4%.
  - Operating profit: ¥24.9bn → ¥10.4bn over the same period (S&P).
- FY3/26 operating profit was ¥10.4bn (−37%) on revenue of ¥442.3bn (−4%).
  - Americas operating profit: ¥1.47bn (−76%), due to higher overheads.
  - China: revenue ¥56.2bn (−20.7%), with lower royalty income.
  - Asia/Europe: still loss-making (¥0.47bn operating loss) after earlier impairments (https://kabutan.jp/news/?b=k202605130305 ; https://japanir.jp/company/company-7313/ir/7313-20260206-01_wp_financial_summary/?ir_category=business_strategy).
- China external revenue (S&P geographic): ¥85.1bn → ¥68.4bn → ¥54.0bn (FY3/24 → FY3/26), −37%. In Q1 FY3/27 the China segment swung to an operating loss.
- Honda dependence: Honda group was 84.3% of sales in FY3/24 (https://edinetdb.jp/company/E02380/main-customers ; automated extraction).
- Guidance cut on 14-Nov-25 from OP ¥16.5bn to ¥12.0bn, citing customer supply-chain issues and delayed launches in the new-customer business. The actual result (¥10.4bn) missed even the cut guidance.
- SG&A is 9.9% of sales vs 6.5% at Toyota Boshoku and 7.6% at Tachi-S, suggesting an overhead gap with Japanese peers.
- Q1 FY3/27: revenue ¥112.8bn (+9.9%), net income ¥1.6bn. FY3/27 guidance: OP ¥13.0bn (2.95% margin), net income ¥8.0bn.

## 7. Governance
- Audit & Supervisory Committee company since Jun-2021, with a voluntary nomination/remuneration committee (2 internal, 3 outside members).
- Board: 11 members in the 2025 integrated report (6 internal / 5 outside, 45%; 3 women). S&P lists 12 current, with 5 independents.
- The board chair is independent (Kaori Matsushita, per S&P), which is unusual.
- New president Eiji Toba (insider since 1994) from 1-Apr-26; Masanari Yasuda left the board in Jun-26.
- 16th MTP (FY3/27–FY3/29) targets:
  - Operating margin 5.0%.
  - ROE above the cost of capital, and P/B of 1x or more "at an early stage".
  - DOE ≥3.5%.
- The 2025 integrated report cites about ¥50bn of shareholder returns over 3 years (the plan period is not confirmed) (https://www.tstech.co.jp/ir/management_policy/mediumterm/).

## 9. Likely management defence (inference)
- "Continuous and stable returns not dependent on performance" (DOE ≥3.5%), with a payout above 100% already, would be presented as proof of shareholder alignment.
- Net cash is needed for new-customer business, EV/seat technology and the India JV, and as a cushion against Honda volume swings.
- The new CEO and the 16th MTP margin target (5%) need time; a reorganisation was implemented on 1-Apr-26.
- The Honda relationship is the core of the franchise, so management would resist any strategic review that threatens it.
