# Sindoh Co., Ltd. (KOSE:A029530) — OWNERSHIP: FAIL

Sector: office imaging (copiers and printers) and 3D printers. Market cap ₩318.5bn (S&P, 2026-10-15) ≈ US$226m at ₩1,410/US$.

**Why it fails**
1. The Woo family group holds **48.69%**: Sindoh SDR (신도에스디알) 22.63%, Chairman Woo Suk-hyung (우석형) 11.78%, Woo Ja-hyung (우자형) 6.41%, Sindoh System (신도시스템) 6.05% and the Gaheon Sindoh Foundation 0.51%. That is 4,907,999 shares (FnGuide, last change 2024-07-19; 2024 business report).
2. In Feb-2026 the company cancelled 364,300 treasury shares (3.61% of 10,080,029). After that, Woo Suk-hyung holds 12.23%, Sindoh SDR 23.48% and Sindoh System 6.27%, and the group about **50.5%** (approx.: 4,907,999 / 9,715,729). Heir Woo Seung-hyup (우승협) holds only 0.18% directly. He controls through Sindoh System (50% owned by him), which owns 30.3% of Sindoh SDR; bizwatch puts his effective control at about 35%.
3. Treasury: 1,289,380 shares = **13.27%** (2026-03-31; this matches S&P's 8,426,349 shares outstanding). **VIP Asset Management is the 3rd-largest holder at 7.28%** (sentv, Jul-2026). VIP is a value fund that has recently run engagement campaigns at Lotte Rental and Daewon Industries; no Sindoh-specific campaign was found. The press flags "주가 누르기" (share-price suppression) concerns around the succession. The FY25 DPS was cut from ₩1,500 to ₩1,000.
4. Context only: price ₩32,800 → ₩32,000 → ₩43,800 → ₩37,800. Approx. TSR 1y −11.4%, 3y +30.6%, 5y +36.6%, against KOSPI 200 at +85.0%, +189.3% and +140.5%. LTM EBIT −₩1.3bn. S&P net cash ₩420bn, about 1.3x market cap. TEV is negative (S&P TEV/EBIT 80.5x is negative-over-negative, n/m); ND/EBITDA −26x (screen).
5. Conclusion: FAIL, with the family around 49–50.5% plus 13% treasury. Classic cash-hoard and succession-discount case. VIP's 7.28% is the only outside block.
