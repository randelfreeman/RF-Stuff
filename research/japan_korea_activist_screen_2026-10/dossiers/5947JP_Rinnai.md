## 5. Balance sheet and capital
- Cash ¥166.8bn plus short-term investments ¥13.6bn = ¥180.4bn. Debt ¥10.1bn. **Net cash ¥170.3bn = 37% of market cap** (S&P, Jun-26).
- Long-term investments: ¥29.2bn. Other long-term assets: ¥67.0bn (composition n/a). Cross-shareholding detail: n/a.
- Excess cash on the company's own rule:
  - The new MTP sets the required cash level at 2.5 months of sales, about ¥98bn on FY3/26 sales.
  - Excess is therefore about **¥82bn (approx.), 18% of market cap**.
- Minority interests: ¥53.3bn. These are partly-owned overseas subsidiaries; this is a structural leak of consolidated profit.
- Buybacks (S&P): about ¥10bn a year: FY3/23 ¥10.1bn, FY3/24 ¥10.2bn, FY3/25 ¥10.0bn, FY3/26 ¥10.0bn (2.76m shares, May–Nov 2025, then cancelled). No FY3/27 programme announced as of the Aug-26 results.
- Payout: DPS ¥100 on EPS ¥259.96 in FY3/26, so 38.5%. Dividends plus buybacks were about 66% of net income (approx.).
- ROE: 8.6% in FY3/26 (company). Equity ratio is very high: common equity ¥437.9bn on assets ¥647.6bn.

## 6. Business and mismanagement evidence
- Geographic revenue, FY3/24 → FY3/26 (S&P):
  - China: ¥76.9bn → ¥74.0bn → ¥65.9bn (−14%).
  - USA: flat (¥53.8bn → ¥56.6bn); profitability kept through production cuts in a falling-sales year.
  - Japan: ¥183.7bn → ¥198.6bn.
- The previous MTP ("New ERA 2025") was expected to miss its final-year targets mainly because of China water heaters (https://www.marketscreener.com/news/rinnai-medium-term-business-plan-2026-2030-3-689kb-ce7f5bd9de8df423).
- Operating margin is improving (9.1% → 9.9% → 10.7%, FY3/24–26) but FY3/27 guidance is flat: OP ¥50.5bn on sales of ¥500bn (10.1%).
- SG&A is 22.1% of sales vs a peer median of 19.9% (Noritz 28.1%, Corona 19.3%, Chofu 18.5%, A.O. Smith 20.4%).
- M&A is accelerating: MT Industrial (Peru, revenue ¥9.8bn) acquired 31-Oct-25. The MTP adds Australia, Mexico and Costa Rica deals, a US Lakes plant and China Fengxian Phase II. M&A discipline risk is rising.
- New MTP "accelerate 2030" (FY3/27–FY3/31):
  - FY3/31 targets: sales ¥620bn, OP ¥70bn, ROE 10%, ROIC 15%.
  - 5-year plan: operating cash flow ¥198.2bn, investment ¥124.1bn, returns ¥105.9bn (https://www.rinnai.co.jp/ir/document/pdf/202603intermediate_term.pdf ; https://finance.logmi.jp/articles/384673).

## 7. Governance
- Company with an Audit & Supervisory Board.
- Board per S&P: 9 directors, of whom 4 are independent (44%): Dochi, Kato (new Jun-26), Ogura, Sato.
- Chair Kenji Hayashi (co-founder family, 5.33%). President Hiroyasu Naito (founder family) on the board since Nov-2005 per S&P (about 21 years). The chair/CEO roles are split, but both are family.
- Representative Director Tsunenori Narita retired in Jun-26 (becomes special advisor); outside director Kamio also retired.
- Takeover defence: none identified. The board rejected Dalton's capital-cost disclosure and buyback proposals in 2025.
- The MTP states an aim to raise "corporate value and P/B" through capital efficiency, but sets no P/B target.

## 9. Likely management defence (inference, using company language)
- Buybacks beyond plan "could distort the financial foundation and constrain necessary growth investments" (the board's 2025 response to Dalton).
- The company says "accelerate 2030" already commits to progressive dividends at about a 40% payout, "flexible buybacks" and a cash rule of 2.5 months of sales.
- On pay and alignment, the company says the "Representative Chairman and President already hold many shares".
- Growth capex and M&A (US, China, Latin America, electrification and heat pumps) are presented as the better use of cash than returns.
