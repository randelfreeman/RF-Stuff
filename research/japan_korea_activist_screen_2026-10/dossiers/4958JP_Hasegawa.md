## 4. Balance sheet and capital

- **Cash, no debt:** cash ¥35.1bn and zero debt (Jun-2026). Long-term investments are ¥11.5bn. Together they equal 33% of market cap; net cash alone is 25%. Equity is ¥135.0bn and LTM ROE is 5.8%.
- **Cross-shareholdings (Sep-2025):** 30 listed holdings with a book value of ¥10.7bn, mostly customers: Lion ¥1.22bn, Kao ¥1.13bn, Ajinomoto ¥0.94bn, Meiji ¥0.82bn, Mandom ¥0.77bn and Morinaga ¥0.76bn, among others. Three holdings were sold in FY9/25 for ¥0.88bn. Ajinomoto, Takara, Osaka Organic and Takasago hold Hasegawa shares in return.
- **Buybacks:**
  - FY9/25 total ¥2.24bn: ¥1.0bn (306,400 shares, Nov-2024 to Apr-2025) plus ¥1.238bn (400,000 shares, 5-Aug-2025).
  - FY9/23–FY9/24: none shown in S&P (n/a).
- **Payout:** FY9/25 payout was 43.7% (policy at the time: about 40%). From FY9/26 the policy is DOE ≥3%, and DPS rose from ¥74 to ¥100, about 52% of LTM EPS of ¥194.
- **Real-estate gains:** n/a.

## 5. Business and mismanagement evidence

- **Mix:** 90% flavours, 10% fragrances. FY9/25 sales by region: Japan ¥37.1bn, US ¥14.8bn, China ¥12.5bn, Asia ex-China ¥7.9bn.
- **Margins slipped:** FY9/25 operating profit margin was 11.6%, down 1.5pt, on higher US and parent-company SG&A. Japan operating profit fell about 20%.
- **US M&A has a poor record:**
  - Flavor Ingredient Holdings (2017): goodwill fully written off, ¥2.28bn.
  - Mission Flavors (2020).
  - Abelei (2024): ¥6.85bn, of which ¥3.29bn goodwill.
  - The US segment made an operating loss of ¥280m on ¥14.8bn of sales in FY9/25.
  - The auditor's key audit matter is the ¥28.7bn carrying value of TH USA, which is 24% of parent assets.
- **Vietnam acquisition:** Hoang Anh (Vietnam) cost ¥4.48bn, of which ¥3.68bn is goodwill and intangibles. That is about 12x annualised Q2 operating profit (approx.).
- **Overhead is high:** SG&A is 25.7% of sales against a peer median of 18.5% (S&P definitions; R&D classification may differ).
- **Margin gap is mainly against Givaudan:** EBIT margin is 11.6% vs Givaudan 17.9%, but above the peer median of 10.5%. The bigger gap is valuation: EV/EBIT of 11.5x vs a peer median of 28x.
- **Large capex plan:** ¥36.8bn over three years (Fukaya plant renewal, a second US plant, a third China plant, a new Malaysia plant) vs LTM capex of ¥4.3bn. Most of the cash pile is earmarked.

## 6. Governance

- **Board:** a company with an audit & supervisory board. 10 directors, 4 of them independent outside directors (40%). 4 statutory auditors, 3 of them outside.
- **Chair and CEO are split.** Takao Umino is Representative Director and Chairman. Kenji Hasegawa has been President & CEO since 1-Oct-2024; he is a former Mizkan Holdings president, and the company says he is not related to the founding family.
- **Committees:** voluntary nomination and remuneration committees, each with an independent majority and an independent chair.
- **Board-only dividends:** the articles let the board decide dividends (Art. 459), which keeps dividend proposals off the AGM agenda. Directors serve one-year terms.
- **Takeover defence:** none found (n/a).
- **TSE "cost of capital" disclosure:** the Nov-2025 dividend policy change was explicitly framed as "management with an awareness of the cost of capital".
