## 6. Business & mismanagement evidence

  FY25 sales were ¥115.1bn (−3.9%).
- **Basic Chemicals is the drag.**
  - 9M FY25 sales ¥34.5bn (−8.2%), OP only ¥0.75bn (−38%, ≈2% margin), hit by imports.
  - Functional Materials earned ¥8.35bn OP on ¥42.7bn of sales in the same period.
  - The company has exited some solvents. Its MTP aims for ¥13.6bn of cumulative 3-year EBITDA from Basic Chemicals.
  - The CEO, Aug-2026: "improving profitability in the basic chemicals segment is a critical management priority".
- **H1 FY26:** sales ¥53.3bn (−9.3%), OP ¥7.5bn (+24.9%), NI ¥5.3bn (+34.3%), on price revisions and electronic-materials demand. FY26 NI guidance is unchanged at ¥8.7bn.
- Overhead is lean: SG&A is 11.2% of sales against a peer median of ≈16.4%. This is not a cost-bloat case. The issues are capital structure and portfolio.

## 9. Likely management defence (inference)
- Management has already acted: payout 50%, DOE ≥5%, a ¥5bn buyback, and an "ongoing capital allocation review" to hit ROE 15%. It will argue that a leveraged recap is unnecessary.
- Basic Chemicals supplies feedstock that is integrated with Functional Materials at its Yokkaichi/Chiba sites. Divestment would break the integration, and restructuring (solvent exits) is already under way.
- Growth capex (refrigerant-oil feedstock capacity, electronic materials) and stable supply require balance-sheet flexibility.
- Strategic Capital's ~18% plus custody holders could swing votes, so expect pre-emptive further returns (a buyback or higher DOE) before the Mar-2027 AGM.
