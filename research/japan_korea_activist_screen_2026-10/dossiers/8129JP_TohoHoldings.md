## 5. Balance sheet & capital
- **Cash:** cash + short-term investments ¥109.1bn against debt ¥5.9bn, so net cash is **¥103.2bn (40% of mkt cap)**. Part of this is seasonal working capital: payables ¥457bn vs receivables ¥358bn + inventory ¥97bn.
- **Investment securities:** ¥69.2bn consolidated (Mar-26), 27% of mkt cap.
- **Policy cross-holdings:**
  - Falling: ¥68.8bn (29.0% of net assets, Mar-21) → ¥41.6bn (16.2%, Mar-25) → **13.8%** (Mar-26), about ¥37.5bn (approx.).
  - Target: below 10% by Mar-29. Management says the remaining holdings are "important business partners" (May-26 call, Pronto).
- **Combined:** net cash + securities ≈ ¥172bn ≈ **66% of mkt cap**.
- **Real estate:** PP&E ¥88.7bn. Parent-only land ¥17.8bn at book (revalued). Unrealised gains n/a. The parent earns ¥4.6bn of rental income.
- **Buybacks:** ¥12.0bn / ¥15.0bn / ¥10.0bn in FY24 / FY25 / FY26 (S&P cash flow). But 3.2m and 4.1m treasury shares were re-issued in FY25 and FY26 to settle conversions of the 2028 convertible bond (CB), which blunted the net share reduction.
- **Returns:**
  - ROE 6.1% (LTM); the company reports 6.6% for FY26.
  - FY26 DPS ¥165, a payout of 60.8% of EPS ¥271.
  - Cash total return in FY26 ≈ 89% (approx.: dividends ¥5.4bn + buybacks ¥10.0bn, vs NI ¥17.3bn).
  - New mid-term plan: total payout ≥100%, DOE ≥4%, shareholder returns ≥¥50bn over FY26–28, DPS ¥180 guided for FY27.

## 6. Business & mismanagement evidence
- **Segments, FY Mar-26:**
  - Wholesale: revenue ¥1,494.9bn, OP ¥16.8bn (1.1%).
  - Dispensing pharmacy: revenue ¥100.5bn, OP **¥1.4bn (1.4%)**. This is sub-scale and a sale candidate.
  - Pharma manufacturing (Kyosomirai Pharma): revenue ¥11.6bn, OP ¥0.28bn.
  - Others: revenue ¥7.0bn, OP ¥0.83bn.
- **Profit falling:** FY26 OP guidance was cut on 28-Apr-26 from ¥20.7bn to ¥16.5bn. FY27 guidance is OP **¥14.8bn**, against a mid-term target of ≥¥30bn (≥1.5% margin) and ROE ≥8% by FY29 ([MTP](https://www.tohohd.co.jp/assets/data/260428-3.pdf)). The previous plan's targets (ROE 8%, P/B >1x) were missed.
- **Deal activity:**
  - Approached **PHC Holdings** (KKR-owned; Bloomberg said the deal could exceed ¥200bn, about 77% of Toho's market cap) on 30-Sep-26, then dropped it on 9-Oct-26 (S&P key developments).
  - Smaller venture-style stakes: Ishin Pharma (majority, Jul-26), Surv BioPharma, Pharmo (30.3% stake, ¥0.9bn). The auditor flagged valuation of unlisted biotech stakes bought above net assets as a key audit matter (yuho).
- **Asset sales:** exited Solasto (tender, May-26) and Ayumi Pharmaceutical (sold to GNI, Jul-26).
- **Compliance (3D's claims):** three bid-rigging sanctions plus the Nihon Univ. Hospital affair, which 3D says cost it some supplier relationships.

## 7. Governance
- **Structure:** company with an Audit & Supervisory Committee.
- **Board:** about 10 directors, 5 independent outside directors (**50%**, approx., from S&P professionals data). 3D says the board "lacks a majority of independent directors".
- **CEO:** Hiromi Edahiro has been President since Jul-2017 (about 9 years). He was also CFO until 2025; Yasuo Sakae is CFO now. No separate chair is listed.
- **Takeover defence:** an "emergency" pill adopted 31-Oct-25, approved by the 2026 AGM. The Tokyo District Court enjoined it on 18-Sep-26, and Toho filed an objection on 6-Oct-26 ([tokyobrief](https://tokyobrief.com/jp/articles/toho-holdings-contests-injunction-share-warrant-defence)).
- **Cost-of-capital disclosure:** cost of equity stated as "about 6–7%" (MTP).

## 8. Catalysts 2025–26
- **27-Jun-25:** AGM; Edahiro re-elected with 70.01% despite 3D's campaign and Glass Lewis's "against" recommendation.
- **31-Oct-25:** pill adopted. 3D filed its large-scale purchase statement in Jan-26 seeking up to 27%.
- **28-Apr-26:** new mid-term plan with 100% total payout; year-end dividend raised to ¥120.
- **26-Jun-26:** pill approved with 54.7%. If 3D (27–29% of votes cast, assumed) voted all its shares against, non-3D support was about 75–77% (approx.).
- **13-Aug-26:** 3D petitioned for an injunction; granted **18-Sep-26**. Toho objected **6-Oct-26**; the objection is pending.
- **30-Sep to 9-Oct-26:** PHC approach opened and abandoned.
- **Next:** objection ruling, then a possible High Court appeal. 3D could then move to 27% and gain a veto over special resolutions (Toho itself puts that threshold at 27.17%).

## 9. Likely management defence (inference)
- "TSR has consistently exceeded peers over 1, 3, 5 and 10 years." Toho said this in Jun-25, and it is true for 5y (+130% vs a +64% peer median).
- "Net cash is working capital needed for stable supply. The new plan already returns ≥100% of earnings and ≥¥50bn."
- "A 27% holder could force short-termist strategies." This is Toho's stated rationale for the pill.
- "Growth through healthcare M&A (the PHC approach) and specialty logistics raises margins toward 1.5%."
