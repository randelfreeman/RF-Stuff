## 6. Business & mismanagement evidence
- **Customer concentration:** Samsung group was 42.0% of FY3/25 sales (¥48.5bn), against 41.5% the year before.
- **Technology Solutions (TS):** FY3/26 sales ¥77.3bn, down 11.1% on OEM demand, a strong yen and US tariffs.
- **Branded Products:** FY3/26 sales ¥32.7bn. It returned to profit after restructuring.
  - S&P CY2025: Branded OI ¥0.45bn on ¥30.2bn of sales (1.5%), against TS OI ¥17.5bn on ¥79.6bn (22%).
  - Corporate costs were −¥5.5bn.
  - Branded swung from +¥8.7bn to −¥4.0bn in FY3/23. FY3/26 impairment of ¥474m on brand assets in Japan, Germany, the US and China.
- **Volatile earnings:** OP was ¥13.0bn (FY22), ¥2.0bn (FY23), ¥7.1bn (FY24), ¥10.2bn (FY25), ¥13.3bn (FY26). FY3/27 guidance: sales ¥110bn, OP ¥14bn.
- SG&A is 26.2% of sales against a peer median of ≈18.5% (Logitech 20.4%, Elecom 27.6%, Synaptics 16.6%, ELAN 7.0%).
- **Related-party M&A:** the ≈¥1.66bn purchase from outside director Nakajima's company (2026-03-31). An internal review committee's opinion dated 2026-01-14 called the price appropriate, according to AVI's deck.

## 9. Likely management defence (inference)
- Chapter 4 has delivered: OP is up 31%, Branded is back in profit, total return is ≥50% with a progressive dividend, and the share count is down 19% since 2022.
- The Samsung partnership, including its 6.24% stake, is a strategic moat rather than a risk. Management will say that OEM volume volatility is cyclical.
- The related-party deal was vetted by an independent review committee and serves the pen-technology and creative ecosystem strategy.
- Votes show majority support. Management will frame AVI's demands as short-termist; the 35% vote against the CEO says otherwise.
