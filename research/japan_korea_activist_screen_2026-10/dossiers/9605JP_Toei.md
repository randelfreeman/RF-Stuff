# Toei Company, Ltd. (TSE:9605) — Film/TV production, cinemas; parent of Toei Animation

- Market cap: ¥368.7bn (US$2.33bn at 158 JPY/USD); S&P TEV ¥363.2bn (2026-10-15)
- **OWNERSHIP: PASS**
  - Largest holder: TV Asahi HD at 19.43%. No holder or group is above 40%.
  - However, the friendly strategic cross-holder bloc totals about 49.4% (see below).
- **Toei Animation (TSE:4816) itself fails the gate.**
  - Toei holds 41.0% of its voting rights (6.6% of that indirectly), and parties "close to or in agreement with" Toei hold another 20.0% (TV Asahi). That is about 61% controlled.
  - Source: Toei FY3/26 yuho, affiliates table.
  - This makes Toei Animation unusable as a target, but it is the core of the Toei Co. holding-company thesis.

## 6. Business & mismanagement evidence
- **Toei Animation dominates.** It is 52% of Toei's LTM revenue (¥96.2bn of ¥185.6bn) and 87% of EBIT (¥31.9bn of ¥36.9bn). Toei's own businesses run at about a 5–6% EBIT margin (approx.).
- **Cross-holdings with no visible return:** 15+ listed names held for "関係強化・協業推進" (strengthening relationships and promoting collaboration). EDINET DB counts 25 policy holdings.
- **Mutual 19–20% holding with TV Asahi,** which trades at a P/B of 0.64.
- **T-Joy buyout (Jul-2025):** Toei bought out the remaining 34.3% (¥3.1bn) by issuing 0.61m new Toei shares, i.e. it paid in an undervalued currency. T-Joy's FY3/25 EBIT was only ¥0.75bn on ¥18.9bn revenue.
- **FY3/26 earnings quality:** net income was lifted by a real-estate sale gain (guidance raised 2025-11-13). FY3/27 guidance is OP ¥28.7bn and NI ¥12.6bn (EPS ¥201.64).
- **MTP to 2033:**
  - Spend: ¥240bn on content and ¥60bn on infrastructure (production ¥36bn, real estate ¥24bn).
  - Targets: 50% of sales overseas, baseline OP of ¥25–40bn, ROE ≥8%.
  - No buyback or cross-holding reduction target.
- **Toei Kaikan (Ginza HQ):** to be redeveloped as an income property. The HQ moved to rented space in Kyobashi in Jul-2025.

## 7. Governance
- **Board:** 14 directors (company with an Audit & Supervisory Committee). S&P labels 6 as independent (43%). In practice independence is doubtful for 3 of them:
  - Hiroshi Hayakawa is **Chairman of TV Asahi HD**; on the Toei board since 2012, age 82.
  - Hirofumi Nomoto is Chairman of Tokyu, which holds 4.61% of Toei; on the board since 2014.
  - Hitoshi Sato is the former president of Tokyu Recreation.
- **Interlocks:**
  - Chairman Noriyuki Tada is an "independent" outside director of TV Asahi HD and a director of Toei Animation.
  - President Fumio Yoshimura (in post since Apr-2023) sits on Toei Animation's board.
  - Senior MD Koichi Wada is a statutory auditor of Toei Animation.
- **Parent–subsidiary listing:** Toei Animation is listed, with TV Asahi as an "other affiliated company".
- **Takeover defence and TSE P/B disclosure:** n/a. The only cost-of-capital commitment found is the ROE ≥8% target.

## 9. Likely management defence (inference)
- The cross-holdings are said to support IP collaboration with broadcasters and Bandai Namco ("映像事業収益の最大化", maximising earnings from the visual content business). Management would say that selling them damages the content value chain.
- Content is volatile ("ボラティリティの高い映像事業", the highly volatile visual content business), so they will argue the cash and real estate are a stability buffer.
- They have just introduced a 30% payout policy and a ¥240bn growth investment plan, and would say returns are improving.
- Collapsing Toei Animation is a TV Asahi/minority-protection issue, so they would say it cannot be decided by Toei alone.
