- **Market cap:** ¥39.3bn ≈ **US$0.25bn** at 158 JPY/USD. Source: S&P CIQ, 15-Oct-2026, close ¥584. This is on about 67.27m shares, i.e. after the October 2026 placement; see below. On the pre-placement 54.2m shares, market cap at ¥592 would be ¥32.1bn.

- **OWNERSHIP: PASS (technical).** No holder exceeds 40%. However, a strategic and business-partner bloc of about 46% after the placement makes activism impractical; see section 2.

## 6. Business & mismanagement evidence
- **Shrinking earnings.** FY3/26 sales were ¥148.3bn (−12%), down from a ¥188.5bn peak. Operating profit was ¥4.91bn and net income ¥2.46bn, against ¥10.2bn of net income in FY3/23.
- **Q1 FY3/27:** operating profit ¥0.64bn (−64%). Net income of ¥1.91bn was helped by an asset-sale gain. The FY3/27 forecast is operating profit ¥3.4bn and net income ¥3.9bn ([#11](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000264682743-11)).
- **A bet-the-company capex plan.** A new 1.2Mt EAF costs **about ¥95bn**, and up to about ¥105.5bn per the search summary. That is **about 2.4x market cap**. Construction starts in August 2026, operation in 2030 or later, and the company does not expect earnings contribution until 2031. Another ¥35bn is earmarked for maintenance and growth ([AR #480](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000254919028-480), [Yuho #325](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000262413036-325)).
- **Dilutive equity raise at about 0.33x book.** Shares were placed at ¥666 against June-2026 BPS of about ¥2,044 (equity ¥110.8bn / 54.2m shares). Post-deal BPS falls to about **¥1,777 (−13%)** (computed, approx.). This was done instead of a buyback or a market-priced raise, and it entrenched friendly holders.
- **Customer concentration.** Hanwa accounts for ¥29.4bn of sales (about 20%) and ¥11.6bn of purchases (FY3/26) ([#772](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000262413036-772)).

The 2026 vote outcome is n/a ([#434](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000262413036-434), [#435](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000262413036-435)).

## 9. Likely management defence (inference)
- It will argue that the EAF requires capital, citing "growth investment priority until FY2030; consider strengthening returns after completion" ([AR #487](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000254919028-487)).
- It will present the Hanwa/YODOKO placement as "strategic cooperation" that secures slab offtake and completion of the project.
- It will hold that a payout of at least 30% is adequate given the cyclical trough.
- It has structural blocks to fall back on: about 46% friendly holders and a 20%-trigger rights plan. Practical activist leverage is low unless a campaign targets the EAF capital allocation via a public letter or vote-no on the rights plan.
