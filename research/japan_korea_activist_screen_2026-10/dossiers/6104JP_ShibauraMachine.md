## 6. Business & mismanagement evidence
- **Earnings collapsed after a China capex boom.** FY3/26 sales fell 21.0% to ¥132.8bn and operating profit fell 69% to ¥4.37bn, an OPM of 3.3%. The cause was lower sales of lithium-ion battery separator-film lines in China ([#399](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000262668073-399)).
- **Segment results** (FY3/26 vs FY3/25) ([#1041](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000262668073-1041)):

  | Segment | Sales FY3/26 | Profit FY3/26 (margin) | Profit FY3/25 (margin) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Moulding machines | ¥98.9bn | ¥2.74bn (2.8%) | ¥14.15bn (10.3%) |
  | Machine tools | ¥25.4bn | ¥2.07bn (8.1%) | n/a |
  | Control machinery | ¥8.3bn | **−¥0.49bn** loss | n/a |

  Control machinery is sub-scale.
- **Q1 FY3/27** (to Jun-2026): sales ¥21.1bn (−31.8%) and an operating loss of **¥0.65bn**. Full-year guidance is unchanged at sales ¥137bn, OP ¥4.2bn (3.1%) and net income ¥2.0bn ([#3](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000264369545-3)).
- **The mid-term plan will miss badly.** "中計2026" targeted sales of ¥200bn, OPM of 10.0% and **ROE of at least 9.5% for FY3/27** ([AR](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000227646542-61)). Guidance implies sales 31% below target, an OPM of 3.1% and ROE of about 2%. The long-term 2030 goal of a "¥300bn sales company" remains in place ([#144](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000262668073-144)).
- **Serial bolt-on M&A with cash:**
  - A 100% acquisition in May 2025 for ¥528m, which produced ¥118m of negative goodwill.
  - An 80% European injection-moulding acquisition in November 2025.
  - The ~¥24bn Moore deal, about 21% of market cap ([#988](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000262668073-988), [#1000](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000262668073-1000)).
- **Cash flow:** FY3/26 FCF was **−¥11.2bn** because working capital rose.

## 7. Governance
- **Board:** 12 directors, of whom 7 are outside (58%) and 2 are women. The company uses the audit & supervisory committee structure ([#591](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000262668073-591)).
- **CEO:** Shigetomo Sakamoto, President since February 2020 and CEO since June 2021 (6.7 years). He is also chairman of JMTBA. He is joined by CFO and Representative Director Hiroaki Ota, formerly of GCA/Houlihan Lokey.
- **Takeover defence:** the company keeps a "basic policy" on large-scale purchases, which commits it to requesting information and taking measures "permitted by law/articles". The text explicitly cites greenmail and "temporary high dividends via asset sales" ([#585](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000262668073-585)). No standing rights plan is referenced in the excerpt.
- **Cost of capital:** the integrated report states that "profitability above the cost of capital is indispensable" ([AR FY3/26 #470](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000266917882-470)). There is no numeric P/B target (n/a).

## 9. Likely management defence (inference)
- It will argue the downturn is cyclical (China separator-film demand) and that non-China moulding, machine tools and precision machines are growing. It will say orders rose year on year in Q1 ([#10](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000264617549-10)).
- It will point to the ¥140 DPS being kept despite EPS of ¥43.5, past buybacks and cancellations, and P/B of about 1.0x as evidence of shareholder alignment.
- It will justify the Moore acquisition with the plan's "portfolio transformation" and "globalising ultra-precision" language, and will resist large special returns by citing growth M&A.
- It will cite the large-purchase policy and the 2020 Murakami episode to frame aggressive asks as "greenmail / asset-stripping".
