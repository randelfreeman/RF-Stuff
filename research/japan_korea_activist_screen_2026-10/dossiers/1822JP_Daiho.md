# Daiho Corporation (TSE:1822) — OWNERSHIP: FAIL

Sector: marine and civil construction (TOPIX-17 Construction & Materials, ETF 1619). Market cap ¥66.9bn (S&P, 2026-10-14), about US$424m at ¥158/US$. TEV ¥49.7bn.

**Why it fails**
1. **Parent company: Aso Corporation (麻生, Iizuka, Fukuoka) holds 44,530k shares, 50.12% of shares excluding treasury** (有報 FY3/2026, register at 31-Mar-2026). The 有報 names Aso as 親会社等. Aso took control in Jul-2022 through an ≈¥40bn third-party allotment of 8.5m new shares, which took it to 50.74%. The proceeds funded a self-tender that bought out City Index Eleventh (Murakami-related), which held 38.66% at end-2021, or 42.43% with joint holders. Aso stepped in as white knight.
2. **Top 5 (31-Mar-2026, ex-treasury basis):**

| Holder | % | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Aso Corp. | 50.12 | Parent / strategic |
| ATRA Co. (Shibuya; Murakami-family vehicle, rep. Aya Nomura) | 13.18 | Activist-related |
| Sumitomo Realty & Development | 3.37 | Strategic |
| Master Trust (trust a/c) | 3.14 | Index |
| Custody Bank (trust a/c) | 1.88 | Index |

   ATRA became a 主要株主 during FY3/2026. A change report filed 2026-05-28 (No.18, by Minami-Aoyama Fudosan with ATRA and M Holdings as joint holders) shows the group cut to **8.65%** (ATRA 7,537k shares = 8.34%). In other words, the Murakami group is selling down.
3. **Treasury:** 1,569,830 shares (1.74% of 90,415,815 issued), plus 530,100 shares in the directors' stock trust. 3,000k treasury shares were cancelled on 2026-06-23. There was a 5:1 split on 2025-04-01.
4. **Price return, context only (S&P closes ¥773 → ¥766 → ¥803 → ¥794):** 5y +2.7%, 3y +3.7%, 1y −1.1%. TSR is n/a because S&P's DPS series is not split-consistent around the 2025 5:1 split. TOPIX returned +29.0%, +78.8% and +105.2% over the same periods. Peers were not computed.
5. **Conclusion:** a >50% parent-controlled subsidiary. The only activist-style angle is a parent buy-out or squeeze-out of minorities, not a proxy campaign. FAIL.
