# Mirai Industry Co., Ltd. (TSE:7931) — OWNERSHIP: PASS (high stable-holder share)

**Sector:** electrical installation materials. Resin conduits, boxes and pipes (電材及び管材), plus wiring devices through subsidiary Jimbo Electric. Based in Gifu. Over 90% of sales are domestic. TSE Prime and Nagoya. FY ends 20 March.
**Market cap:** ¥58.0bn ≈ US$367m at ¥158/US$ (S&P, 2026-10-14). TEV ¥37.1bn.

## 6. Business & mismanagement evidence
- **Profit has stalled.** OP ¥7.33bn (FY3/24) → ¥6.90bn → ¥6.72bn (FY3/26). Sales were ¥45.7bn (+1.2%).
- FY3/27 guidance: sales ¥48.5bn (+6.3%) but OP ¥6.23bn (−7.3%), on cost inflation. This is despite Q1 OP rising 31.5% to ¥1.95bn.
- **Segments (FY3/26):**

| Electrical materials & pipes | 34.7 | 5.99 | ≈17% |
| Wiring devices (Jimbo Electric) | 8.0 | 0.87 | ≈11% |
| Other: molds/machines, telecom, cable TV | 2.9 external | 0.71 | — |

  The "other" segment includes sub-scale non-core holdings: Mirai Communication Network (71%) and Amixcom CATV (83%).
- **Overhead:** SG&A is 23.8% of sales against a peer median of ≈17.9%. Margins are still high because of gross margin (38.7%).
- **Capital inefficiency:** cash plus deposits are about half of market cap, and nearly a third of the shares sit in treasury without cancellation.
- **Control lapse:** on 2026-05-11 the company corrected its FY3/26 Tanshin for an error in the cost-of-sales ratio used in intra-group eliminations.

## 9. Likely management defence (inference)
- Its "常に考える" (always think) culture, with employee motivation as the engine, leads management to see the employee ESOP and shareholding (≈10.6%) as stakeholders who are aligned with it.
- Cash is held for capex: automated warehouses and new production lines at Ogaki, Ibaraki and Kumamoto (FY3/26 capex ¥3.5bn).
- Management will cite the new 50% payout / DOE 3% floor and the 2023 dividend tripling as evidence of shareholder focus.
- The stable shareholder base (≈42% friendly bloc) and 80% equity ratio reduce any urgency. The treasury stock may be framed as "flexibility for M&A or ESOP".
