## 9. Likely management defence (inference)
- "Stable dividends while securing cash flows for proactive long-term business expansion." This is the company's dividend-policy wording.
- "Higher payouts would impair financial stability and hinder about ¥18.5bn of growth and business-continuity investment." This is the board's 2026 wording.
- "Capital efficiency is adequate: ROE has generally been above benchmark except in the latest year." This is a TipRanks summary of the board's 2026 response. Separately, Tsurumi's price TSR does beat TOPIX over 1, 3 and 5 years.
- "The founding-family-led quality culture underpins a 13% EBIT margin."
