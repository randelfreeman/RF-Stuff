# oRo Co., Ltd. (TSE:3983) — Cloud ERP (ZAC) / marketing services

- Market cap: ¥28.8bn (US$0.18bn at 158 JPY/USD)
- **OWNERSHIP: FAIL** (founder-management bloc 57.1%)

## Why it fails
1. Founder and CEO **Atsushi Kawata holds 39.33%** (6,013,164 shares, register date 2026-06-30). That is just under 40% on its own, and it rises with each buyback and cancellation (38.56% at Dec-2025).
2. Co-founder and director **Yasuhisa Hino** (Senior Managing Executive Officer) holds 17.77% (17.41% at Dec-2025). The two founder-executives together control **57.1%**, a majority that decides every ordinary resolution.
3. Other top holders: Goldman Sachs & Co. Reg 5.47% and Master Trust (trust) 3.91% (2026-06-30). Treasury shares are 311,051, or 2.0% of 15,600,453 issued (S&P Tanshin H1-26).
4. **Activists:** none identified.
5. **Context:** close ¥3,660 (2021-10-15) → ¥1,929 (2026-10-14), −47% in price. DPS (FY21–25: 15/20/30/35/50) gives an approx. 5y TSR of −43.2%. Net cash is ¥8.7bn; EV/EBIT 7.1x; EBIT margin 31.8%. These are attractive metrics, but control makes an activist campaign impractical.
