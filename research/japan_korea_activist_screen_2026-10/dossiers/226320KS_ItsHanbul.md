# It's Hanbul Co., Ltd. (KOSE:A226320) — OWNERSHIP: FAIL

Sector: cosmetics (It's Skin / Hanbul brands); holds a stake in listed Neopharm. Market cap ₩189.7bn (S&P, 2026-10-15) ≈ US$135m at ₩1,410/US$.

**Why it fails**
1. Chairman Lim Byung-chul (임병철) holds **35.25%**. The largest shareholder plus specially related parties hold **61.59%** (13,505,250 shares; Sep-2026 aggregator and earlier press).
2. The related parties are not one bloc. Lim Jin-beom (임진범, nephew and son of the late 임현철) holds 15.73%, brother Lim Sung-chul (임성철) 6.49% and nephew Lim Hyo-jae (임효재) 3.40%, together 25.62%. The chairman's own children, CEO Lim Jin-sung (임진성) and Lim Woo-jae (임우재), hold about 0.36% each. Lim Jin-beom is not in management, and the press calls his stake a "variable" in the succession (thebell, c-journal). Even without the nephews' branch, the group is about 36%. Under the brief's rule the whole related-party group counts, which gives 61.59%.
3. Treasury ≈ 19.7% (approx.: issued shares implied as about 21.93m from 13,505,250 = 61.59%, against 17,616,685 outstanding per S&P; not confirmed in a filing). Activists on the register: none found. A special NTS tax audit linked to the merger has been reported (date unclear).
4. Context only: price ₩21,200 → ₩12,410 → ₩11,650 → ₩11,040. Approx. TSR 1y −2.9%, 3y −5.6%, 5y −43.1%, against KOSPI 200 at +85.0%, +189.3% and +140.5%. LTM EBIT ₩23.9bn (13.9% margin). S&P net cash ₩366bn, about 1.9x market cap, consolidated and partly attributable to Neopharm minorities. TEV is negative (S&P TEV/EBIT −1.7x, n/m); ND/EBITDA −12.6x (screen).
5. Conclusion: FAIL at 61.59%. The family-branch split (nephews about 19%) is the only possible angle and would need re-screening if those holders sell.
