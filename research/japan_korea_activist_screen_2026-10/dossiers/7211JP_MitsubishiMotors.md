## 5. Balance sheet and capital
- **Is the auto business net cash? Yes.** The company reports "Automobiles & Eliminations" net cash of ¥406.2bn at 31-Mar-26 (¥394.5bn a year earlier) (FY2025 results deck, https://www.mitsubishi-motors.com/en/investors/library/pdf/financial/2026/260508_FY25Q4_4e.pdf). The consolidated net debt of ¥106.6bn is therefore finance-subsidiary funding.
  - Auto net cash equals 87% of market cap.
  - Market cap less auto net cash is ¥59bn (approx.).
  - Adding S&P's other TEV items (¥45.4bn, minorities etc.) gives an ex-net-cash EV of about ¥105bn, or roughly 1.3x LTM EBIT (approx.; EBIT includes the finance business).
- Group cash and deposits: ¥438.9bn (Mar-26).
- Long-term investments: ¥51.4bn (S&P, Jun-26). This includes a 19.93% stake in FleetPartners (ASX:FPR), raised in Sep-2025 at A$3.10.
- Cross-shareholdings (政策保有) detail: n/a.
- Buybacks over 3 years: one, ¥68.6bn in Nov-2024 (the Nissan block). None since.
- Payout: DPS ¥10 on EPS ¥7.48 in FY3/26, so 134%. FY3/25 was ¥15 on ¥28.70 (52%).
- ROE: about 1.2% LTM.
- Capital plan to FY3/30: ¥1tn of growth investment and ¥100bn of shareholder returns over 4 years. Policy is a ¥10 DPS floor, progressive dividends and buybacks.

## 6. Business and mismanagement evidence
- Operating margin by fiscal year: FY3/22 4.3%, FY3/23 7.7%, FY3/24 6.8%, FY3/25 5.0%, FY3/26 2.6%.
- FY3/26 operating profit was ¥75.5bn (−46%) and net income ¥10.0bn (−76%). The initial OP guidance of ¥100bn was cut to ¥70bn on 27-Aug-25, citing US tariffs, sales competition and inflation.
- The company's own vision deck admits that some "Challenge 2025" MTP measures "have not progressed as expected".
- Retail volume FY3/26 was 797k units (−5%) (May-2026 earnings call, S&P transcripts).
- Net income has collapsed: ¥168.7bn (FY3/23), ¥154.7bn, ¥41.0bn, ¥10.0bn (FY3/26). Revenue grew 4% in FY3/26.
- SG&A is 9.9% of sales vs a peer median of 11.7%. Overhead is not the issue; the problem is capital allocation and scale.
- 10-year alliance with Nissan: Nikkei Asia called it "a lackluster decade" (https://asia.nikkei.com/business/automobiles/mitsubishi-motors-nissan-alliance-nears-crossroads-after-lackluster-decade).
- MMC stayed out of the Honda–Nissan merger framework. The MoU was signed 23-Dec-24 and terminated 13-Feb-25.

## 9. Likely management defence (inference)
- Cash is needed for the ¥1tn new-model programme (Pajero series), electrification and finance-arm support. The 4-year capital plan already allocates ¥100bn to returns, with a "minimum ¥10 dividend under any circumstances" (Kishiura, May-2026 call).
- In an uncertain environment (tariffs, Middle East) a cautious stance is warranted, and the auto net cash is a buffer.
- The strategic shareholders provide sales, governance and technology support, and their decision to hold signals confidence.
- ROE of 10% is targeted by FY3/30, so the company would argue a structural fix is already under way.
