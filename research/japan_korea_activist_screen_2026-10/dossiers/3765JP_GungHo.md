## 5. Balance sheet & capital
- **Cash:** cash & deposits ¥130.5bn plus securities ¥5.8bn (Dec-25); ¥123.4bn plus ¥5.6bn (Mar-26). No debt.
- **Minorities and investments:** non-controlling interests ¥28.3bn (Gravity). S&P long-term investments ¥16.4bn.
- **Buybacks:**
  - FY22 ¥5.0bn, FY23 ¥5.0bn, FY24 ¥9.9bn, FY25 ¥5.0bn.
  - Feb–Apr 2026: ¥5.0bn (1.98m shares).
  - Treasury cancellations: ¥12.1bn (FY24), ¥41.5bn (FY25), about 16m shares (2026).
- **Dividends:** FY25 DPS ¥90 (¥4.89bn total) is **349% of EPS** (¥25.79).
- **New policy (2026-02-13):** DOE 4% and payout ≥50%. Per the summary of the company's 2026-02-13 management-policy document (EDINET DB summary seen via search): parent cash about ¥70bn, about ¥50bn needed for development over 5 years, about ¥20bn for growth and returns.
- **ROE:** 2.1% (LTM).

## 6. Business & mismanagement evidence
- **Earnings collapse:**
  - Net income: FY21 ¥22.9bn → FY22 19.0 → FY23 16.4 → FY24 11.2 → FY25 ¥1.4bn.
  - FY25 OP was ¥5.1bn (FY24 ¥17.5bn) on sales of ¥93.2bn (FY24 ¥103.6bn).
  - SG&A was ¥37.3bn, or 40% of sales and 88% of gross profit.
- **Board's own admission (Feb-2025):** "despite significant investments, they have not produced a hit title in years". It used this to justify keeping the cash.
- **Dependence on Puzzle & Dragons:** revenue by title is not disclosed (n/a). SC's 2026 proposal to require disclosure was rejected.
- **Pay:** SC says Morishita's pay rose from ¥120m to ¥340m over a decade while profits fell. FY25 individual pay: n/a.
- **Gravity:** a NASDAQ "controlled company". 58.4% of Gravity's 2025 licence and royalty revenue comes from GungHo, a related-party flow.
- **Index exit:** dropped from the S&P Japan 500 (2026-03-23).
- **Current trading:** H1-26 sales ¥51.8bn, NI ¥3.5bn (H1-25: ¥2.5bn).

## 7. Governance
- **Board:** 10 directors, 5 independent (50%). Company with an Audit & Supervisory Board (3 members, 2 outside).
- **Chair:** Kazuki Morishita, not independent; he was CEO for about 22 years until 2026-02-01 and is now Chairman and Chief Development Officer.
- **CEO:** Kazuya Sakai since 2026-02-01; director since 2005 and former CFO. He is also an executive director of Gravity.
- **Pay structure:** a Nomination & Remuneration Committee exists. Performance-linked restricted stock was approved in 2026.
- **Takeover defence:** the supermajority dismissal clause was removed in Sep-2025. No poison pill found (n/a).
- **Cost of capital:** a proposal to require cost-of-capital disclosure was rejected in 2026.

## 9. Likely management defence (inference)
- Management has already changed (Morishita no longer represents the company) and returns now exceed 100% of earnings (DOE 4%, payout ≥50%, buybacks and cancellations).
- They argue game development is high-risk and needs a cash buffer (their own 2025 argument).
- They present the Sony Music alliance as the growth path, combining IP with entertainment know-how. Sony's 22.9% will be presented as a supportive long-term anchor.
- They would say Gravity is a strategic, controlled subsidiary that contributes profit.
