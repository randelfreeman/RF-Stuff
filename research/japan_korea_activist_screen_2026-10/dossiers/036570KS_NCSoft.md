## 5. Balance sheet and capital (Jun-2026)
- **Liquidity:** cash ₩671bn, ST investments ₩582bn and trading securities ₩606bn, **₩1.86tn** in total.
- **Investments:** LT investments ₩525bn. They include **5,842,800 Netmarble shares (6.9%)**, carried at ₩281.9bn (Dec-25) and worth ₩198bn at ₩33,900.
- **Debt:** bonds ₩170bn and leases ₩196bn. S&P net debt is −₩1.49tn.
- **Cash and investments vs market cap:** S&P net cash plus LT investments at book is ≈ ₩2.02tn, **approx. 52% of market cap**.
- **Real estate:**
  - Net PP&E is ₩1.13tn.
  - The Samseong-dong NC Tower I was sold for ₩443.5bn (signed Jul-2025, transfer scheduled for Aug-2025) to fund the Pangyo Global RDI Center. That project has a ₩580bn budget and is due for completion in 2027.
  - Unrealised real-estate gains: n/a.
- **Goodwill:** ₩407bn, after the mobile-casual M&A.
- **Treasury activity:**
  - The FY2024 treasury acquisition of ₩175.8bn came largely from appraisal-rights purchases tied to the spin-offs, not from a buyback programme.
  - 410,000 shares (₩126.9bn, 1.9%) were cancelled on 24-Mar-2025.
  - There was no buyback or cancellation in Q1-26.
- **Payout:**
  - Policy for 2025–27 is 30% of NI excluding one-offs.
  - The FY2025 dividend was ₩22.3bn (DPS ₩1,150), only **6.4% of reported FY25 NI of ₩347bn**.
  - Cash dividends paid fell from ₩135.7bn (2023) to ₩63.6bn (2024) and ₩28.3bn (2025), on a parent basis.
- **ROE:** LTM 17.4%, inflated by the gains.

## 6. Business and mismanagement evidence
- **Collapse:**
  - Revenue went from ₩2.57tn (FY22) to ₩1.51tn (FY25), down 41%.
  - OP went from ₩559bn to −₩109bn (FY24) and then to ₩8.1bn (FY25).
  - R&D stayed at 22–27% of revenue (₩467bn in FY23, ₩325bn in FY25).
- **Restructuring by spin-off:**
  - NC QA and NC IDS were spun off in Oct-2024.
  - FirstSpark Games (TL), BigFire Games (LLL), NC AI and Ludius Games were spun off in Feb-2025.
  - These shrink the parent, but all six remain 100%-owned.
- **The turnaround is real but concentrated:**
  - Aion 2 (launched Nov-2025 in Korea and Taiwan) passed ₩160bn of billings in 3 months and booked ₩136.8bn of revenue in Q1-26.
  - Lineage Classic reached ₩192.4bn of billings in 90 days.
  - Q2-26 revenue was ₩770.5bn (+101% YoY) and OP ₩173.9bn (23% margin).
  - Aion 2's global launch is planned for Q3-2026.
  - The LTM EBIT margin of 13.5% is still less than half the peer median of 29.7%.
- **Mobile-casual M&A spree:** about **₩449bn in about 4 months**:
  - Indygo/Lihuhu, 67% for ₩147.6bn (closed 30-Jan-2026)
  - JustPlay, 70% for ₩301.6bn (closing Apr-2026)
  - Springcomes, 80% (price n/a)
- **Economics of the mobile-casual segment:** Q2 revenue was ₩169.7bn, but the segment's marketing spend was ₩132bn, including JustPlay user rewards. *Inference:* this is low-margin growth bought with cash that could have gone to buybacks.
- **Small investments:** a long tail of VC fund and minority stakes (Big Game Studio, Mystill, Dexa, Dynamis One and others) in the 2025 business report.

## 7. Governance
- **Leadership:** co-CEOs are founder Kim Taek-jin and Park Byung-moo (Q2-26 call). CEO tenure dates: n/a.
- **Board:** size and % independent were n/a in the sources read.
- **Ownership dynamics:** the founder holds 12.4%, and NPS, the Saudi holder and Netmarble together hold about 28.6%. *Inference:* coalition-building is feasible, and no single bloc can block it.

## 8. Recent events and catalysts
- **Jul-2025:** NC Tower I sale for ₩443.5bn.
- **19-Dec-2025:** Lihuhu deal approved (closed 30-Jan-2026).
- **10-Feb-2026:** Q4 call; Aion 2 global launch targeted for Q3.
- **10-Mar-2026:** JustPlay deal (₩301.6bn) and Veroplay (EU subsidiary) set up.
- **May-2026:** treasury falls to 9.62% after employee grants.
- **1-Jul-2026:** NPS stake rises to 10.25%.
- **11-Aug-2026:** Q2 results show the second-highest quarterly revenue in NC's history. Management guides to 10 new IPs, with 2028–29 revenue coming from 2027 launches.
- **Catalysts:** Aion 2 global results, the cancellation of the 9.6% treasury, and the Netmarble cross-holding.

## 9. Likely management defence (inference)
- "Back to basics" has worked: record PC revenue and a 23% margin in Q2-26. Investors should judge the company after the Aion 2 global launch and 10 new IPs.
- Treasury shares are a currency for M&A and employee compensation. NC already cancelled 1.9% and pays 30% of adjusted NI.
- Mobile casual (JustPlay, Lihuhu) diversifies away from Lineage dependence and the Korean market. NA/EU rose to 27% of revenue.
- The net-cash buffer funds the Pangyo RDI Center (₩580bn) and the pipeline.
