## 6. Business and mismanagement evidence
- FY3/26 segments (S&P): Printing Solutions ¥1,029.5bn, 11.7% OP margin; Visual Communications ¥181.4bn, 6.8%; Manufacturing-related & Wearables ¥206.1bn, 5.2%. Unallocated −¥59.8bn plus other operating expense −¥34.2bn.
- **Fiery (acquired Dec-2024 for US$568.7m**, S&P transaction 1898030451): a ¥25.9bn goodwill impairment followed within about 15 months ([#20](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000259532591-20)). FY3/26 net income fell 67% to ¥18.2bn and Q4 was a ¥17.2bn net loss.
- FY3/26 business profit was ¥83.8bn (5.9% of ¥1,413bn sales), against Brother's EBIT margin of 12.9%.
- The 14.2% of shares held in treasury is idle capital.
- Robotics and watches (Orient merged in Feb-2026) are sub-scale non-core candidates; their segment detail is n/a.

## 8. Why +76% in the last year (events)
- **13-Mar-2026: new long-term vision and MTP Phase 1 (FY2026–28).** It sets ROIC of 8% by FY2028 ([#240](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_T000003678808-240)), a DOE floor of 3% and ¥80bn of buybacks. Analysts called the return policy the "biggest change" ([#405](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_T000003678808-405)).
- 1-May-2026: FY3/27 guidance of sales ¥1,450bn / business profit ¥90bn / net income ¥59bn, a rebound from the impairment year. The shares rose 15.2% that day ([MarketsMojo](https://www.marketsmojo.com/news/stocks-in-action/seiko-epson-corp-hits-day-high-with-1516-surge-in-stock-price-3982209)).
- 5-Aug-2026: guidance raised to ¥1,510bn / BP ¥105bn / NI ¥69bn (EPS ¥215) ([#7](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000264572956-7)), plus the ¥30bn buyback. Q1 net income was ¥13.0bn vs ¥6.6bn a year earlier.
- 2-Sep-2026: a US IEEPA tariff refund of US$109m (≈¥17bn) to be booked in Q2 (S&P keydev 2019256081).
- There is also an inkjet-for-semiconductor-packaging partnership with Manz Asia (Mar-2026).
- Conclusion: the rally came from a re-rating on earnings recovery and capital-policy change, **not an activist** (none found).

## 9. Likely management defence (inference)
