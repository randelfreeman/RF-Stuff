### 6b. Ownership and voting restrictions (critical for takeover analysis)

**Legal basis:** the *Eldorado Nuclear Limited Reorganization and Divestiture Act* (Canada) ("ENL Act") and *The Saskatchewan Mining Development Corporation Reorganization Act*, both as amended. The restrictions are implemented through Cameco's articles. The articles hold the binding definitions of "resident", "non-resident", "control" and "beneficial ownership". [v, 2025 AIF (19 Mar 2026), 2024 AIF, 2021 AIF]

| Restriction | Limit | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Individual **non-resident** of Canada (alone or with associates) holding or controlling shares/securities carrying votes to elect directors | **Max 15%** of the votes that may be cast to elect directors (raised from 5% by the 2002 ENL Act amendment) | [v] (2021 AIF; 2010 circular; 2025 AIF; 2003 AIF) |
| Individual **resident** of Canada (alone or with associates) | **Max 25%** of the votes that may be cast to elect directors | [u] (2010 circular; consistent with structure) |
| **Aggregate non-resident voting** at any shareholder meeting | Non-resident votes are counted and **pro-rated down so they do not exceed 25% of total votes cast** (raised from 20% in 2002). This caps votes, not ownership: non-residents can own well over 25% of shares, but their votes are scaled down | [v] (2011 circular; 2016 6-K; 2025 AIF; 2003 AIF) |
| Has the vote cap been applied? | Yes. Cameco "has in past years, including 2017, limited the counting of non-resident votes" | [v] (2017 40-F/AIF filing) |
| Issue/transfer block | Cameco **cannot issue or register a transfer** of voting shares that would breach the resident or non-resident limits | [v] (2025 AIF) |
| Enforcement | Shareholders must make residency/ownership declarations; nominees (banks, brokers) declare for beneficial holders; a holder with no declaration may be **deemed non-resident**. Cameco may **suspend voting rights, forfeit dividends, refuse issue/transfer, and require disposal** of excess shares | [v] (2021 AIF) |
| Purpose | Set by the federal government at Cameco's 1988 formation (Eldorado Nuclear + SMDC merger) to keep Cameco Canadian-controlled | [v] (2018 6-K) |
| Other ENL Act requirements | Head office/registered office in Saskatchewan and related constraints. **Not verified in this search set** | [m] |

**Takeover implications [d]:**
1. No foreign bidder can acquire more than 15% of voting control, or have its votes counted above the 25% aggregate non-resident cap, without **an Act of Parliament amending the ENL Act**. That makes a foreign takeover a political decision, not a shareholder one.
2. Even a Canadian bidder is limited to 25% unless the legislation is changed.
3. The Investment Canada Act (net-benefit and national-security review; Canada's 2022 critical-minerals SOE policy) is a second layer [m, not verified here].
4. Practically, Cameco is takeover-proof absent federal and Saskatchewan government sponsorship.
