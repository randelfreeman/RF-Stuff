*Report date: 3 November 2026. Latest reported period: Q3 2026 (released 30 Oct 2026; MD&A dated 29 Oct 2026).*

**Currency.** Cameco reports under IFRS in **Canadian dollars (C$)**. Every figure here is in C$ millions unless it is labelled US$ or given per unit. Uranium realized prices are given in both US$/lb and C$/lb. Fuel-services prices are in C$/kgU.

**Tagging key**
- **[v]** verified: quoted from a primary Cameco document (news release, MD&A, 6-K or 40-F) in a search extract, or cross-checked by arithmetic or a second source.
- **[u]** single secondary source.
- **[d]** derived by this analyst (the method is shown).
- **[m]** analyst memory, not verified. Used sparingly and flagged.
- **NF** not found within the research budget.

**Method caveat (important).** WebFetch and curl were blocked. All data came from at most 28 web searches, and the search tool returns **machine-summarised extracts** of pages, not raw tables. So even [v] items have passed through a summariser. Before publication, tie the key numbers back to the source PDFs (Q3 2026 MD&A, 2025 annual MD&A and the 40-F exhibits linked in Sources). The Cameco quarterly figures reconcile closely with each other (quarters sum to the annual totals within ±C$2m), which supports the core P&L series.

**Commentary**
1. **Contract-book lag.** Cameco's 2026 realized price (~US$66/lb) is about 25% below spot. The sensitivity table shows the gap closing only gradually: at US$100 spot, realized prices rise from 68 (2026) to 87 (2030). Earnings leverage to term prices is back-end loaded into 2028–2030.
2. **Tier-one operating friction.** McArthur River/Key Lake missed its original 18 Mlb (100%) in 2025, at 15.1. In 2026 the Key Lake mill outage cut the Cameco-share range to 10.0–10.5 Mlb. With purchases and fixed costs spread over fewer pounds, unit costs rose to about C$67–70/lb (Q3: C$72/lb). This is the main source of the 2026 earnings miss.
3. **Westinghouse normalisation.** 2025's C$780m share included Dukovany one-offs. The 2026 initial guide of C$370–430m sits below the stated 6–10% CAGR trajectory and is contingent on US Government definitive agreements. This creates upside optionality, but 2026 guidance needs verifying (see the §3c caveat).
4. **Balance sheet.** The balance sheet is conservative: about C$1.0bn of debentures, an undrawn C$1.0bn revolver now extended to 2030, and DBRS BBB. The next refinancing is Series H (C$400m) in Oct 2027.
5. **Cigar Lake consolidation.** The July 2026 TEPCO exit lifted Cameco's Cigar Lake interest to about 57.4%, roughly +2.9pp of attributable production. That is about +0.5 Mlb/yr at 18 Mlb (100%) [d].
