Business overview
CONTEXT

Coupang at a glance: Korea's owned-logistics e-commerce leader with a loss-making second engine

  • 1P retail plus a 3P marketplace with fulfilment (FLC), ads and a WOW membership. Free next-day "Rocket Delivery" since ~2014
  • More than 78m sq ft of fulfilment and logistics space; S&P lists 108,000 employees
