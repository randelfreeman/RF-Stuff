
# 21. Adversarial deep dive: bull case, bear case, pre-mortem, contrarian view

## 21.1 Bull case

1. **The moat survived a stress test.**
   - Customers left for alternatives with ample time to try them, then came back "not splitting their prior spend" ([#267]({S}T000003779883-267), [#268]({S}T000003779883-268)).
   - WOW is at an all-time high ([#271]({S}T000003779883-271)).
   - Physical-network scale (78m sq ft) and cohort economics ([#69]({S}T000003779883-69)) are intact. Management: "nothing has structurally changed" ([#252]({S}T000003779883-252)).
2. **Margin recovery is mostly arithmetic.**
   - The 390bp PC margin compression came from volume below a fixed-cost demand curve, plus one-off promotions and marketing ([#170]({S}T000003779883-170), [#204]({S}T000003779883-204)). Management calls this reversible ([#261]({S}T000003779883-261)).
   - Each 100bp of PC margin is ~US$0.35bn of EBITDA [d].
   - Vouchers have finished (mid-April 2026) and marketing steps down in 2027, so 2027 comparisons are easy.
3. **Secular growth levers.**
   - Online is still 41.5% of Korean retail ([#305]({S}NONSEC000256804358-305)), and Coupang's wallet share is below US-peer levels ([#80]({S}T000003779883-80)).
   - Fresh, FLC and ads are margin-accretive.
   - Taiwan is reaching Dawn Delivery in one year against four in Korea ([#99]({S}T000003779883-99)).
   - Farfetch has turned to positive economics ([#121]({S}T000003665452-121)).
4. **Potential earnings surprises.**
   - KRW mean reversion: a 10% move is worth ~US$3.5-4bn of revenue.
   - A faster DO loss cut (Q2 losses down US$110m QoQ already).
   - Insurance recoveries on the Incheon fire.
   - A successful PIPC appeal (US$410m accrued).
5. **Capital allocation.** Buying back ~3% of the shares in H1'26 at ~US$19.5 ([#369]({S}SEC000264559057-369)), with US$907m of authorisation left. The founder's incentives favour long-term value.

## 21.2 Bear case: three risks that could permanently impair the business

1. **The Korean regulatory regime turns hostile to the model, not just the incident.** Four threats at once:
   - A night-delivery ban would end Dawn Delivery ([#862]({S}SEC000255973354-862)).
   - Forced unbundling of Eats from WOW ([#2009]({S}SEC000255973354-2009)).
   - Shortened merchant settlement periods that drain the payables float ([#619]({S}SEC000255973354-619)).
   - Escalating fines and criminal referrals ([#465]({S}SEC000264559057-465)). Cumulative fines are already ~US$531m [d].
2. **Competitive leakage becomes permanent.**
   - Naver's asset-light alliance (CJ Logistics, Kurly) plus its Plus membership (with Netflix) offers "good enough" speed without the capex. Naver gained GMV and members in January 2026 ([#298]({S}T000003646016-298)).
   - Chinese platforms exceed 10m users ([#344]({S}T000003711263-344)).
   - Coupang's 2026 promotions suggest pricing power has weakened ([#167]({S}T000003779883-167)).
3. **The capital-allocation and cash engine breaks.**
   - FCF has fallen from US$1.76bn (FY23) to ~US$0.1bn (LTM).
   - Taiwan losses run at ~US$1bn a year with no published hurdle.
   - Buybacks are being funded with debt.
   - If margins stall at ~5% PC and the float shrinks, Coupang becomes a low-return retailer valued like Lotte Shopping or E-MART (0.4-1.0x sales).

**Margin compression and revenue deceleration risk.** PC growth slowed from +18% cc (Q3'25) to +5-8% cc (H1'26). Management's Q3 guide implies roughly 6-7% PC growth in constant currency ([#432]({S}T000003779883-432)). If the non-returning cohort never comes back and promotions persist, the PC margin could settle at ~6%, not 8%+.

**High expectations.** Not on revenue: the stock is at 0.79x sales. But the market still assumes a **return to profitability**. On FY27E the stock trades at 113x GAAP EPS and 16.6x adjusted EBITDA. Any further slip in the recovery date (it has slipped once already) would hurt.

## 21.3 Pre-mortem: "It is October 2027 and CPNG is at US$9. What went wrong?"

1. The Q4'26 guide for 2027 showed PC margin recovering only to ~6.5%, not "pre-incident", because promotions had to stay to defend share against Naver and Chinese platforms.
2. The KFTC ordered Eats unbundled from WOW. WOW churn rose and Eats volume fell.
3. The settlement-period rule took ~US$0.7-1.0bn of payables float out in 2027. Combined with capex and the PIPC payment, the company moved to net debt and stopped buybacks.
4. The KRW stayed weak and USD revenue growth stayed in mid-single digits.
5. A night-delivery bill progressed in the National Assembly.
6. Taiwan losses did not fall, and the market started valuing DO at a negative number.

*Early-warning indicators:* PC margin in Q4'26 and Q1'27; Q4'26 WOW and active-customer disclosure; the KFTC decision; payables days after Dec-2026; the size and pace of buybacks; DO loss guidance for 2027.

## 21.4 Are multiples too high?

On trough numbers, yes: 32x LTM adjusted EBITDA, n.m. P/E, 0.3% FCF yield. On normalised numbers, no. FY25-level adjusted EBITDA margins (4.3%) on FY27E sales of US$41bn would give ~US$1.8bn of EBITDA, or ~16x [d]. At the FY28E base case the stock is at 10.7x adjusted EBITDA and 0.61x sales. The question is not whether the multiple is high. It is whether margins normalise.

## 21.5 The contrarian view: what the market refuses to see

The market is treating Coupang's USD-reported 2026 as the new run-rate. In fact, 2026 combines four temporary distortions:

1. A KRW at a 15-year low (-7pts on PC growth in Q2).
2. ~US$1.2bn of compensation vouchers netted against revenue.
3. A one-off reacquisition marketing spend.
4. US$410m of fines and ~US$246m of fire exposure.

Strip these out and Coupang is still a ~10% constant-currency grower, with 24.7m customers and its highest-ever WOW membership.

A second under-appreciated point: **the Delivery Hero/Baemin deal re-prices Korean delivery assets.** Uber is paying ~1.1x sales for a business whose crown jewel competes head-on with Coupang Eats ([#1089]({S}SEC000263635795-1089)), while Coupang as a whole trades at 0.79x sales.

The contrarian *bear* view the market also refuses to see is the working-capital time bomb. Coupang's negative-working-capital model (CCC of about -57 days) has funded growth, and Korea's post-TMON settlement reforms are aimed squarely at it ([#619]({S}SEC000255973354-619)).

# 24. The short-seller's case (forensic and adversarial)

**What could structurally break how Coupang makes money?**

- The economics depend on three things: a regulator tolerant of overnight labour, bundled memberships, and long settlement terms.
- Each is now in play: night-delivery ban ([#861]({S}SEC000255973354-861)), Eats-WOW bundling ([#2008]({S}SEC000255973354-2008)), and the EFTA / Large-Scale Retail Business Act reforms ([#619]({S}SEC000255973354-619)).
- Remove the payables float and the business needs external capital to grow.

**Concentration**

- Over 90% of revenue is in Korea ([#1858]({S}SEC000255973354-1858)). Product Commerce is ~86% of revenue and more than 100% of adjusted EBITDA.
- A single country's consumer mood, regulator and currency drive the entire equity.
- If Korean shoppers' default shifts to Naver's ecosystem (search, then membership, then delivery), there is no second leg. Taiwan and Farfetch are loss-making or marginal.

**Why the moat is weaker than bulls think**

- Switching costs are behavioural, not contractual.
- Naver gained members and GMV within weeks of the breach ([#298]({S}T000003646016-298)).
- Coupang needed promotions and marketing to win customers back ([#420]({S}T000003779883-420)).
- Regulation works *against* incumbency.

**The most dangerous competitor: NAVER**

- It owns the top of the funnel (search and price comparison), payments and a membership with Netflix.
- It now delivers dawn grocery via Kurly and CJ Logistics without Coupang's capex ([#321]({S}NONSEC000256804358-321), [#326]({S}NONSEC000256804358-326)).
- Bulls underestimate it because they benchmark Coupang against retailers, not against the traffic gatekeeper.

**Worst capital allocation**

- About US$1bn a year of Developing Offerings losses ([#56]({S}SEC000255972090-56)).
- The Japan v1 exit ([#72]({S}T000002800142-72)).
- Debt-funded buybacks at ~US$27.6 in 2025 and ~US$19.5 in 2026, against a price now of US$15.82.
- The Greenoaks buyout at ~US$122-140m against US$70m contributed ([#1956]({S}SEC000237408246-1956), [#2052]({S}SEC000255973354-2052)).

**Related parties, accounting and incentives**

- **Greenoaks.** Director Mehta is its Managing Partner ([#2050]({S}SEC000255973354-2050)).
- **The "administrative fines" add-back.** It is new in 2026 and judgment-based, despite a recurring pattern of fines ([#339]({S}SEC000264559057-339)).
- **Vouchers.** US$1.2bn was netted against revenue with no accrual at announcement ([#16]({S}SEC000264559057-16)).
- **SBC.** US$475m in FY25, more than net income, with US$973m unvested ([#1886]({S}SEC000255973354-1886)).
- **Insider selling plans after the breach.** CFO, GC and CAO adopted plans in March-May 2026 ([#300]({S}SEC000259645823-300), [#303]({S}SEC000259645823-303), [#294]({S}SEC000264559057-294)).
- **Founder selling.** Bom Kim adopted a 15m-share plan in 2024 ([#311]({S}SEC000232383623-311)).

**What must hold for today's price [d].** At US$15.82 the EV is ~US$28bn. Assume ~10% revenue CAGR to ~US$54bn in 2030, 20x terminal FCF and a 10% discount rate. Coupang then needs a **~3.8% FCF margin by 2030**. That is a return to FY23-24 levels (7.2% and 3.3%), against 0.3% today.

**If growth disappoints by 20-30% [d]**

| Scenario | Revenue CAGR | 2030 revenue | 2030 FCF margin | Implied value per share | Change |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Growth -20% | 8% | ~US$50bn | 3.8% | ~US$14.5 | -8% |
| Growth -30%, margin also slips | 7% | ~US$48bn | 3.0% | ~US$11 | -30% |

The stock is far more sensitive to margin than to growth.

**The single scenario that would permanently impair the business.** A Korean regulatory package that simultaneously:

- bans night deliveries;
- forces membership unbundling;
- shortens settlement terms; and
- reclassifies delivery partners as employees.

That combination would convert Coupang into a low-margin, capital-hungry retailer worth ~0.4-0.5x sales (~US$8-10 a share). **Plausibility: low to moderate (~10-15%).** Each element exists today, but the full combination would require a political shift against a 100,000-plus employer at a time of weak consumption.
