## Q1. Consolidated P&L, FY2021–FY2025, 1H FY2025 and 1H FY2026

### Takeaway
- **Revenue:** rose from ¥340.5bn in FY2021 to ¥474.6bn in FY2025.
- **Operating income:** rose from ¥58.8bn to a record ¥95.8bn. OP margin went from 17.3% to 20.2%.
- **Net income:** peaked at ¥65.9bn in FY2024, lifted by large Q4 extraordinary gains. It then fell 10.6% to ¥58.9bn in FY2025, because gains on securities sales shrank and equity-method results swung to a ¥6.9bn loss.
- **Business profit (事業利益) definition:**
  - FY2020–FY2024: operating income + equity-method income (stated verbatim in the Yuho).
  - From FY2025: also includes gains/losses on sale of non-current assets "etc.".
  - FY2025 business profit was ¥89.4bn, up 12.7% from the restated ¥79.3bn.
- **1H FY2026:** operating income +17.1% (¥39.9bn) on revenue −6.9% (¥194.4bn); NI +13.7%.

**Business-profit (事業利益) definition**
- **FY2020–FY2024 definition.** The Yuho states verbatim: 「連結営業利益に持分法投資損益を加えた「連結事業利益」を目標とする利益指標として採用」. In other words, business profit = consolidated OI + equity-method investment gain/loss. Sources: [Y20-pol](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yuukimiyo/stdata-jp/HEAD/8/88040/2021/BusinessPolicyEtc) and [Y22-pol](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yuukimiyo/stdata-jp/HEAD/8/88040/2023/BusinessPolicyEtc).
- **Change effective FY2025.** The new definition is OI + equity-method income + "etc.", where "etc." includes gain/loss on sale of non-current assets. The stated rationale is "to respond to the flexible sale of non-current assets and the diversification of investment schemes in Overseas business". Source: FY2025 materials via search ([EP25](https://pdf.irpocket.com/C8804/doF3/FpJe/bqVe.pdf)).
- **FY2025 versus restated FY2024.** FY2025 business profit was ¥89,419m against a restated FY2024 figure of ¥79,326m, i.e. +12.7%. Two separate search extractions agree ([T25](https://pdf.irpocket.com/C8804/YpwX/Bm8F/l2Ip.pdf), [EP25](https://pdf.irpocket.com/C8804/doF3/FpJe/bqVe.pdf)) [V].
- **Implied "other items" in the new definition [D]:**
  - FY2025: 89,419 − 95,763 − (−6,870) = **+526**.
  - FY2024 restated: 79,326 − 79,670 − 819 = **−1,163**.
  - Under the old definition, FY2024 business profit was 80,489.

### Inferences
- **Why FY2025 NI fell while OI rose 20%.**
  - The non-operating line deteriorated: ordinary income − OI went from −¥7.9bn to −¥17.6bn.
  - Of that, equity method contributed −¥7.7bn (+819 → −6,870).
  - The rest of the non-operating line went from about −¥8.8bn to −¥10.7bn [D]. This is most likely higher net interest expense on a larger debt load and higher rates. That is an inference: interest expense itself was not retrieved.
  - Extraordinary gains also shrank, per Shikiho's 「有証売却益減」.
- **FY2024 NI was not a clean run-rate.**
  - NI/ordinary income was 91.9%, against roughly 65–75% in the other years.
  - Q4 NI exceeded Q4 ordinary income.
  - Two December 2024 material-event reports were filed.
  - Together these strongly suggest about ¥20bn+ of pre-tax extraordinary gains, most plausibly sales of cross-shareholdings (政策保有株式) [E]. Analysts should normalise FY2024 EPS of ¥315.5.
- **FY2021 non-operating gap.** Ordinary income was ¥12.5bn below OI, an unusually large gap. It most likely reflects equity-method losses and/or overseas write-downs. This is unverified; see Gaps.
- **Seasonality.** Earnings are heavily 2H/Q4-weighted (FY2025 Q4 OI ¥45.2bn of ¥95.8bn). 1H FY2026 progress of about 38% of OI guidance is therefore normal for this company.
- **Payout.** The payout ratio rose to 37% in FY2025 as DPS rose despite lower EPS. This is consistent with a dividend floor or progressive policy, but the policy text was not retrieved.

### Inferences
- **Inventory in OCF.** In J-GAAP, purchases of land and development costs for condos and investor-sale properties (販売用不動産) flow through operating CF as inventory increases, while sales proceeds come back through OCF. - **Investing CF.** Investing CF captures fixed-asset (rental property) development, e.g. FY2024 capex of ¥125.7bn. "True" asset recycling is split across both lines: fixed-asset sales sit in investing CF, while sales of for-sale real estate sit in operating CF.
