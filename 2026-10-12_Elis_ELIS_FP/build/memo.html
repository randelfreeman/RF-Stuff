What it does and why it is defensible
  • Rents, launders and delivers flat linen (~45%), workwear (~36%) and hygiene / well-being equipment (~17%) in 31 countries; 70% of revenue outside France.
  • Customers: healthcare ~30%, hospitality 27%, industry ~26%, trade & services ~18%; top-10 clients <10% of revenue, largest contract <1%.
  • Moat = route density: a full van and a full laundry. #1, #2 or #3 in most countries and #1 in more than half.
  • Weak points: labour is ~60% of costs and energy 6–7% of revenue; capex runs at ~18% of sales; cost inflation is passed on only with a lag.
Economic profile: ROIC ~7% on EBITA after tax; free cash flow after leases is only ~21% of EBITDA.

Key risks
Downside
Four risks that could make the stock cheap for a reason
01
Organic growth slows below 3%
Pricing normalises after the inflation years while volumes in French hospitality and German industry stay soft. Each 1pt ≈ €1.5/share.
02
Labour and energy outrun indexation
Labour is ~60% of costs; 2026 minimum wages +23% in Colombia, +13% in Mexico, a large rise in Germany; energy shocks (2022, 2026) hit margins for 2–3 quarters before price catches up.
03
A large leveraging acquisition
US entry ambitions (2024 Vestis talks) cost 21% in four days. A big deal would reset leverage above 3x and pause buybacks.
04
Capital intensity hides low returns
ROIC is ~7%: the short-seller view is that cheapness is deserved; FCF after leases is only ~21% of EBITDA.
Pre-mortem (Oct-2027, the stock is €15): Q3/Q4 organic growth printed ~2%, Mexico and Germany worsened, the OCEANE was not converted, BWSA stopped buying and a 2027 French election shock cut the multiple to 9x. Mitigant: stop-review below €17.

Questions
What we would ask the CEO and the Chairman before sizing up
CEO (Xavier Martiré)
  • If the share price is below €20.8 on the soft-call date, what is plan B for leverage, the 2027 buyback and the €380m OCEANE?
  • What organic growth do the record 2026 signings imply for 2027, and how much is price vs volume?
  • Why did H1 FCF turn negative, and what gets H2 to €390m+?
  • Under what conditions would you again pursue a large US or European acquisition, at what leverage?
  • How much of the 2026 minimum-wage increases in Germany, the UK and LatAm is still unrecovered?
Chairman of the Supervisory Board
  • Is BWSA/BWGI a long-term investor capped at 25%, or a potential controlling holder?
  • What would the board do with an unsolicited bid at €30–35?
  • How is management's long-term incentive linked to ROIC and FCF per share, not revenue?
  • After the Sep-2026 Management Board renewal and Matthieu Lecharny's departure, what is the succession plan?
  • Would the board support cancelling treasury shares rather than holding ~10% of capital?
