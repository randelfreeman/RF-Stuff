## 2. Large moves, last 5 years (file `model/D_big_moves.csv`)

**Method** [calc]:
- Returns are close-to-close.
- **Single-day moves:** |return| ≥ 5%, for trading days after 2021-10-08.
- **Episodes:** every start/end pair 1–20 trading days apart with |return| ≥ 10% is a candidate. Candidates are ranked by magnitude and picked greedily so that no two overlap (they may share an endpoint).
- **Months:** month-end to month-end returns of |return| ≥ 10%.
- **Peer context:** peer returns are measured over the same dates (US calendar; last close on or before each date). For single days, a "to t+1" column is also given because US markets close after Paris.
- **Causes** come from ProntoNLP documents (Elis calls, filings and releases; peer filings and calls).

### 2.4 Key episodes explained

**(a) US M&A scare, Sep–Oct 2024 (M&A; the largest stock-specific move).**
- *Price action* [calc]: −15.66% on 6-Sep-2024 (open −9%, low €19.12), then −21.1% over 5→10-Sep. On 4-Oct-2024 the stock rose +10.0% (intraday high +15%), and +17.0% over 2→18-Oct. Peers were flat both times.
- *Vestis link* [calc]: Vestis rose +11.7% in the 5-Sep US session, after the Paris close and before Elis's fall the next morning.
- *Elis disclosure (29-Oct-2024 Q3 release):* "the Group held exploratory conversations with two US market players during the third quarter; both discussions were terminated at the beginning of October, as neither would have allowed Elis to complete a transaction that would have been in line with its strict financial discipline" [#1](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000232051849-1).
- *Later comments:* at the FY2024 call the CEO cited "the U.S. negotiation" as proof of M&A discipline [#458](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_T000003394029-458). At the 2025 CMD he said "you saw last year that we were ready to evaluate the potential acquisition of major targets like Vestis" [#3277](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_T000003468571-3277), and that Vestis is now "not at all an option for Elis" [#3109](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_T000003468571-3109).
- *Vestis side:* Vestis later disclosed "preliminary proposals received for potential acquisitions of Vestis" and retained Centerview [#127](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_T000003316682-127).
- *Gap:* the press item that triggered the 6-Sep move is not available; the News corpus is not licensed.
- *Investment read* [inference]: the market heavily penalised the risk of a large, leveraging US deal. Investment-grade status (S&P BBB- since Nov-2023 [#232](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_T000003054377-232)) and the subsequent capital-return policy reduced that overhang.

**(b) Capital-allocation pivot, 6-Mar-2025 (+9.05%; episode +18.2% from 19-Feb to 18-Mar-2025 while peers fell 7.1%)** [calc].
- FY2024 results came with a new cash allocation policy: bolt-ons of €50–150m a year, keeping the IG rating, leverage cut capped at about 0.1x a year, and "remaining cash… to improve shareholder return" [#133](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000257201966-133).
- A €150m buyback started that day, and a €0.45 dividend was proposed [#4](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000257201966-4).
- Management explicitly said valuation "does not fully reflect the group strength and potential" [#377](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_T000003394029-377).

**(c) French politics, June 2024 (−13.5% from 6→28-Jun while peers rose 2.7%; −6.0% on 14-Jun)** [calc].
- H1 2024 call: "Our stock price lost around 15% since the dissolution of the French National Assembly, which seems exaggerated…" [#81](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_T000003194583-81).
- Elis generates about 30% of revenue in France [#155](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_NONSEC000227525215-155).
- French politics recurs as a risk: analysts raised the impact of the new French budget at the FY2024 call [#407](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_T000003394029-407), and the 2027 election came up on the H1 2026 call [#457](https://gam.prontonlp.com/#/ref/$SENTID_T000003774119-457).

## 5. Data gaps and caveats
- **Corpora not licensed:** News and Investment Research (logs -016 and -017); the Macro corpus is also unavailable. Macro triggers (Omicron, the Ukraine-war start date, US CPI, tariff dates, French government crises) are therefore supported only where Elis or peer documents mention them. Otherwise they are labelled [u] or "not identified".
- **No ProntoNLP price history before 2019.** The IPO price (€13) is from filings; there is no 2015–2018 price performance.
- **Partial dividend adjustment of the ProntoNLP series** (§1.3). Pre-2024 levels look about 4–7% below the prices quoted in filings.
- **Peer lines:** Rentokil and Sodexo are USD lines, so peer comparisons include FX. Vestis data starts in Sep-2023.
- **Exact announcement dates not available** for: the leak of Elis's US talks (Sep-2024), their termination (early Oct-2024), the announcement of the €500m 2026 buyback (it started 6-Jan-2026; the stock rose +3.8% on 7-Jan [calc]), and the start of the Middle East conflict (Feb/Mar-2026).

- **No Elis documents after 30-Jul-2026**, so the Aug–Sep 2026 weakness and the 21-Sep-2026 anomaly remain unexplained.
