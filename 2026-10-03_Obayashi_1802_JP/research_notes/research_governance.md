### Founder vs professional manager
Hybrid: Chairman Takeo Obayashi is family; the presidents since 2011 (Shiraishi, Hasuwa, Sato) are salaried managers; family holding is modest (2.46%) - no controlling shareholder. Family name still on the Chairman role -> influence over succession (Sato selection in Dec 2024 was announced with Obayashi remaining chair).

### Controversies / red flags
- Linear Chuo Shinkansen bid-rigging: Obayashi self-reported to JFTC (leniency applicant, per Nikkei) and admitted; indicted Dec 2017 era/2018; Tokyo District Court fined the company JPY200m (Oct/Nov 2021 reporting; Shimizu JPY180m); JFTC surcharge order 22 Dec 2020 of about JPY3.1bn (Obayashi) / JPY1.2bn (Shimizu) = JPY4.3bn total; cease-and-desist to four majors. Obayashi and Shimizu got a 4-month (120 day) business suspension in Tokyo from 2 Feb (2021) per Global Construction Review. Sources: https://www.nikkei.com/article/DGXZQODG215PQ0R21C20A2000000/ ; https://www.nikkei.com/article/DGXMZO36759630S8A021C1CC1000/ ; https://www.globalconstructionreview.com/shimizu-obayashi-hit-four-month-suspension-bid-rig/ (JPY figures from Nikkei; the 43億円 and 2億円 figures consistent.) ## 6. STRATEGY (10 years) AND ASSESSMENT
- MTP 2017 (2017-2021): domestic profit rebuilding; hit by Linear scandal (2018) and civil margin issues.
- MTP 2022 (2022-2026): "Strengthening the business foundation and practicing transformation"; targets: operating income JPY100bn+, EPS JPY100+, equity ratio ~40%, ROIC >=5%; addendum 13 May 2024 raised capital efficiency (ROE >=10% by FY2026, equity JPY1tn, strategic reduction). Actuals: operating profit JPY194.6bn in FY2025 vs JPY100bn target, ROE 14.4%, ROIC 8.4% -> targets exceeded well ahead of schedule (helped by building margins, change orders, real estate gains). Guidance for FY2026 lower (JPY180bn). Assessment: success on profit/ROE, largely cyclical + pricing; cross-holding reduction on track.
- Overseas: US platform (Webcor, Kraemer, MWH, GCON) a deliberate scale-up in water and data center/semiconductor construction; Asia stable. Early positive contributions; integration risk and price of GCON/MWH not disclosed.
- Wood/ESG: Cypress Sunadaya, wood high-rise projects (Port Plus Yokohama, details n/a); renewable energy (biomass/wind) and Smart Construction/robotics: not researched in detail (n/a).
- Space elevator concept (Obayashi 2012 plan, 2050 target) is R&D marketing, not financial.
