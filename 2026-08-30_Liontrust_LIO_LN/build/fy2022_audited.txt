*** THE TSR HURDLE COLLAPSE — the key finding ***
 OLD LTIP (awards to FY2022): absolute TSR "below 10% PER ANNUM nil; at 10% p.a. 10-20% vests;
   at 15% PER ANNUM AND ABOVE 100% vests". Over three years 15% p.a. = 1.15^3 = 52% CUMULATIVE for max.
 2024 DRP LTIP (FY2026 award): absolute TSR threshold 10% TOTAL (10% payout), maximum 15% TOTAL (100% payout)
   over the three-year period.
 => The hurdle for MAXIMUM vesting fell from ~52% cumulative to 15% cumulative — a cut of roughly 71%.

*** THREE LTIP STRUCTURES IN THREE POLICIES ***
 1. PRE-2022 policy: FIXED FACE VALUE. Ions face value GBP870,250 every year FY2019-FY2022; share count varied
    inversely with price: FY2017 59,070 @280.6p (GBP828,750); FY2018 73,628 @450.2p (GBP828,750);
    FY2019 147,607 @589.6p; FY2020 114,206 @762.0p; FY2021 61,719 @1410.0p; FY2022 53,389 @1630.0p.
    Face value equated to 250% (Ions) and 175% (Abrol) of base remuneration.
 2. 2022 DRP: FIXED NUMBER OF SHARES (153,130 / 112,295), value varies with price.
    Committee's own words: "The Committee specifically designed the LTIP as a fixed number of shares...
    No adjustment will be made on account of short term share price volatility which AVOIDS ANY POTENTIAL
    WINDFALL GAINS from the LTIP based on award date. Decoupling the size of the LTIP award from a percentage
    of salary also REMOVES ANY RATCHET AFFECT."
 3. 2024 DRP: PERCENTAGE OF SALARY (350% / 250% max) — reintroduces exactly the ratchet the 2022 Committee
    designed the plan to avoid.
