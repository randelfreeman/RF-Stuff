## Medium-term plan and long-term vision (search extracts)
- **Medium-term business plan FY2025–FY2027:** FY2027 targets — business profit ¥95.0bn, net income attributable to owners ¥60.0bn; ROE ~10% and ROA ~4% over the plan period. Basic policy: "realise growth acceleration and improved capital efficiency by building a robust and flexible business portfolio". Capital-efficiency levers: diversify the rental portfolio, reduce cross-shareholdings, strategic sales of fixed assets, accelerate/expand asset-turnover (develop-to-sell) business, disciplined balance-sheet control; plus "steady profit growth", "improved capital efficiency", "enhanced shareholder returns". Business profit definition revised to include gains/losses on fixed-asset sales (and expanded overseas investment structures). Sources: edinetdb.jp mid-term plan page https://edinetdb.jp/company/E03859/midterm-plans/ ; Integrated Report 2025 https://recruit.tatemono.com/ir/library/pdf/integrated_2025_all.pdf (search extracts).
- **Long-term vision 「次世代デベロッパーへ」 ("Becoming a Next-Generation Developer"), published 2020:** target year 2030; business profit ¥120bn; balanced profit mix anchored by stable rental income with attention to capital efficiency. Source: https://tatemono.com/company/pdf/management_v2024_20200302.pdf ; Integrated Report 2026 (search extracts).
