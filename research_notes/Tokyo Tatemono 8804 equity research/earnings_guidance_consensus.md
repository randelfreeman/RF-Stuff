## Q1. Results events Feb 2025 – Aug 2026: headline numbers, guidance changes, management emphasis

### Takeaway
After a guided profit dip in FY2025 (net income guided down ~17% at the start of the year), Tokyo Tatemono beat and raised guidance repeatedly: at Q3 FY2025 (Nov 2025), with record operating profit (OP) for FY2025 (Feb 2026), and again at 1H FY2026 (Aug 2026), when it raised FY2026 OP to ¥105.5bn, net income (NI) to ¥65.0bn and DPS to ¥126. The main drivers have moved from condo deliveries (FY2024) to investor property sales and inflation-linked office rent increases (FY2025–26). Residential profit is in a timing-driven trough in 1H FY2026.

### Inferences
- **Recurring pattern:** guidance is set conservatively and raised during the year. FY2025 was raised at Q3, and the final result still beat the revised OP by 3.5% and NI by 1.5%. FY2026 was raised one quarter earlier (at 1H).
- **Mix shift:** the profit engine moved from condos (FY2024 Residential ≈ ¥38bn **(derived)**) to Building/investor sales (FY2025 Building ¥67bn; 1H FY2026 ¥40bn). Residential is in a delivery trough in 1H FY2026 but has a large land bank.
- **Seasonality is extreme:** Q4 FY2025 alone was ≈50% of FY2025 NI **(derived)**. Reading Q1 alone is therefore unreliable, as Q1 FY2026 (−60% NI, outlook held) showed.

## Q2. Sentiment analysis: tone by event and trajectory

### Takeaway
Tone moved from **cautious/neutral (mid-2025)** to **positive (Nov 2025 – Feb 2026)**, dipped to **neutral** at Q1 FY2026 (a timing-driven miss with the outlook held), and became **the most positive of the period at 1H FY2026** (raised guidance, higher DPS, "firm confidence" in rent growth, MTP targets one year early). The weak points shifted toward residential delivery timing, overseas equity-method income, corporate costs and financing costs.

### Inferences
- **Scoring method:** −2 negative, −1 cautious, 0 neutral, +1 positive, +2 strongly positive. Scores reflect whether guidance and DPS were raised, held or cut, the YoY direction of profit, and the language used in quoted management or press text. The trajectory runs +0.5 → 0 → −0.5 → +1 → +1.5 → 0 → +2.
- **Themes shifted over time:**
  - 2025: capital policy (payout, ROE).
  - Late 2025 – early 2026: investor property-sale gains and Yaesu rent increases.
  - Aug 2026: inflation-linked rent growth as a structural story, plus early MTP achievement.
- **Caveats grew as tone improved:**
  - Residential timing (Q1 FY2026).
  - Overseas equity-method income (cut at 1H FY2026).
  - Corporate costs.
  - A rising gap between OP and ordinary income: ¥17.6bn in FY2025 vs ¥22.0bn implied by revised FY2026 guidance **(derived)**, which in practice means interest and other non-operating costs.

  The quality of the upgrade therefore leans on property-sale gains, which are cyclical and rate-sensitive, more than on recurring income.

## Q3. Latest result (1H FY2026, with Q1 FY2026 context): vs expectations, drivers, margins, outlook, balance sheet, market reaction
