## 1. Does 078070 consolidate 264450? — YES (in full), and that is the whole trick

**Yes.** Ubiquoss Holdings' consolidated financial statements **fully consolidate** Ubiquoss Inc. (264450) — line-by-line, 100% of its revenue/assets — with a large **non-controlling interest (NCI)**. 264450 is listed as a 연결대상 종속회사 (consolidated subsidiary), alongside Nexight, BiLab, Ubiquoss Investment and Ubiquoss USA.

**But the holdco owns far less than 100% of the economics:**

| Ownership of 264450 | Stake | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 078070 direct | **~31.96%** | Largest single shareholder |
| + Nexight (100%-owned sub) | +3.0% | |
| + Ubiquoss Investment (100%-owned sub) | +3.0% | |
| **Group look-through** | **~38%** | Economic interest attributable to 078070 |
| + other special-related parties | to ~41.5% | Basis for *control* |

Consolidation is justified by **de-facto control** (dominant largest-shareholder position + the holding-company structure) under K-IFRS 1110, **not** by a majority stake. The consequence, which drives everything below:

> **~62% of everything inside 264450 — its ₩155bn+ of revenue, its ~₩80bn cash pile, its ~₩196bn of equity — shows up on the holdco's *consolidated* balance sheet but belongs to *minority* shareholders, not to 078070's owners.** Any analysis that reads consolidated cash/earnings as if the holdco owned them is wrong by ~60%.

## 2. Pure holdco (별도 / parent-only) net cash — the number you actually asked for

### 2.1 The correction that matters
The often-quoted **"~₩80bn net cash / current ratio 804% / debt-free"** describes **Ubiquoss Inc. (264450), the operating company** — explicitly "the best balance sheet *among telecom-equipment makers*." It is **not** holdco cash. The holdco's economic claim on that ₩80bn is only **~32–38% ≈ ₩25–30bn** (the rest is NCI).

### 2.2 What the holdco actually holds on a parent-only basis
078070 is a **pure holding company**: its 별도 revenue is only ~₩4.0bn/quarter (~₩16bn/yr), of which **~₩2.4bn/quarter is dividend income** from subsidiaries, the remainder mostly **rental income** (it owns the HQ/property). Group **consolidated liabilities are just ₩31.1bn on ₩387.7bn assets (D/E ~8.7%)** — a pure holdco of this shape carries little or no borrowing.

| Holdco parent-only (별도) | Estimate | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Borrowings / financial debt | **≈ ₩0 (effectively debt-free)** | High (group barely levered; holdco is a pure shell) |
| Own cash + financial investments | **≈ ₩30–50bn** | **Estimate** — corroborated by the "Financial-asset ~20% of NAV" bucket (≈ ₩40bn) |
| **Pure holdco net cash (별도)** | **≈ +₩30–50bn (net cash, no net debt)** | Estimate — *exact 별도 cash line requires the DART filing, which the data proxy blocked* |

**Two ways to state it:**
- **Pure parent-only net cash:** **≈ +₩30–50bn**, effectively **debt-free**. This is the holdco's *own* dry powder — the pool it says it will redeploy from "Financial assets (20%)" into Growth/Seed.
- **Look-through net cash** (holdco's economic share of *all* group cash): ≈ ₩40bn (own) + ~38% × ₩80bn (264450) + small Nexight ≈ **~₩70bn**. Still far below the ~₩80–100bn one would wrongly infer from the consolidated balance sheet.

> **Bottom line on the balance sheet:** 078070 has **no net debt** at any level. But "pure" holdco net cash is **~₩30–50bn, not ~₩80bn** — the big cash number lives in the 32–38%-owned opco.

## 5. So — is there a free lunch? (the "what you pay for the stubs" test)
- Market cap **~₩185bn** − holdco 별도 net cash **~₩40bn** − 264450 stake at market **~₩48bn** = **~₩97bn** implied for **Nexight + Ubiquoss Investment + BiLab + real estate**.
- Base-case value of those stubs ≈ ₩70 + 20 + 8 + 20 = **~₩118bn** > ₩97bn.
- **Implied discount ≈ ₩21bn (~11–14% of cap)** at base marks — a **discount, but a moderate one**, not the "everything for free" story. Nexight has to be worth **>₩70bn** for the discount to be compelling.

## 6. Corrections to the first note
1. **Holdco net cash is NOT ~40% of market cap.** That figure double-counted the opco's ₩80bn cash. **Pure parent-only net cash ≈ ₩30–50bn (~16–27% of cap)**; the ₩80bn belongs to the 32–38%-owned 264450.
2. **Share price / count.** 078070 has **~17.0–17.6m shares** at **~₩10,900** (market cap ~₩181–194bn) — the earlier "₩17,500" was erroneous.
3. **New positive:** both entities are **buying back and cancelling stock** — 078070 cancelled ₩3.44bn (545,929 sh, 3.10%, May-2026) plus a prior ~₩3.4bn tranche; 264450 cancelled ₩2.01bn (Oct-2025). This is a **genuine, repeated shareholder-return signal** that softens (not eliminates) the "cash-hoarding control vehicle" bear point.
